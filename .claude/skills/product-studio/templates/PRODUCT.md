# Product

Durable product truth for anyone building this product, people and agents alike. No visual
direction here: that lives in DESIGN.md. Mark anything inferred with `assumption:` so the owner
can confirm it.

## Purpose

What the product is for, in one or two sentences.

## Users

| Who | How often | Context of use |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. founders of small companies | daily, hours at a time | at a desk, many windows open, keyboard first |

## Top jobs

The three to five things people do most often, most frequent first. Each one becomes an essential
journey in `lab.config.mjs`.

1. job, in the person's words
2. ...
3. ...

## Platform

Where it runs (macOS desktop, Windows desktop, web, mobile web), the minimum window size, the
primary theme, and the platform conventions it must honour.

## The one idea

The point of view that makes this product different from the other tools in its category, in one
sentence. Every surface's concept card should trace back to it.

## Principles

Three to five product principles that settle trade-offs, e.g. "the keyboard path is never longer
than the pointer path", "nothing is lost: every action can be undone".

## Constraints

Technical, legal or business limits that shape design (offline support, data residency,
accessibility commitments, supported locales).

## Accessibility and inclusion

The level committed to (e.g. WCAG 2.2 AA), assistive technology to test with, and any known gaps.
