# Research: what the best already do

The point is not a mood board. Each pattern you bring back answers a hole from `holes.md` or
sharpens the direction, and fits what this product says it is (and is not).

## Where to look, in order of trust

1. **Shipped products in the same category.** Their own sites, help centres, changelogs and
   keyboard-shortcut pages describe behaviour exactly; public screenshots and launch videos show
   it. For notes, for example: Bear, Apple Notes, Craft, Obsidian, Reflect, Capacities, Heptabase,
   Tana, Notion's editor, Linear's documents. For tasks: Things, Linear, Todoist, Height. Pick the
   ones known for craft, not for size.
2. **Adjacent categories that solved the same problem.** Command palettes (Raycast, Linear),
   inline linking (Roam-style backlinks, Arc), quiet notifications (Things), editors (iA Writer).
3. **Design galleries and concept work.** Public pages on Mobbin, Dribbble and Behance, design
   write-ups and case studies. Concepts are inspiration, not evidence: an unshipped shot has never
   met a hundredth use. Say which is which.
4. **Writing about why.** Teardown posts, design-team blogs, talks. They explain the reasoning a
   screenshot hides.

Use web search for the list, then the built-in browser to look at pages. Respect sign-in walls:
if a gallery needs an account, use its public pages or move on.

## What to capture

For each product, a short entry in `inspiration.md`:

```
### <Product> (shipped | concept)  <source URL>
Does well:  the two or three things that matter for this module
Pattern:    the specific interaction, in your own words
Answers:    hole ids from holes.md (or "direction")
Fits us:    yes / partly / no, against the product brief and the house system, in one line
```

Save screenshots you take into the workspace's `inspiration/` folder for your own reference. They
never reach a critic and never go into the artifact: the artifact links to the source and
describes the pattern in your words.

## The comparison

One table in `inspiration.md`, carried into the artifact's Why tab: the module today and three to
five best-in-class products, scored 1 to 5 on the dimensions this module lives or dies by. For a
notes module: capture speed, writing feel, organisation without ceremony, finding things, linking
to the rest of the work, every-state care, keyboard path. Choose the dimensions from the module's
three most frequent jobs in `brief.md`, not from a generic list. Give a one-line reason for every
score of 5 and every score of 1.

## Guardrails

- Take patterns, not looks. The house system decides how it looks.
- A pattern the product brief rules out (for example databases, a graph view, AI inside the app
  when the brief says AI is external) is recorded as "doesn't fit us" and dropped.
- Never copy text, illustrations or icons from a source into the artifact.
