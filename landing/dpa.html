Data processing

Data processing agreement

When you use Moduo for your business and put other people’s personal data into it, you decide what happens to that data and we process it for you. This agreement sets out how, as Article 28 of the GDPR requires. It’s part of our terms.

Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version

  • For the personal data in your workspaces, you’re the controller and we’re your processor.
  • We use it only to run Moduo for you. We don’t sell it or train AI models on it.
  • Our database is in London, UK. Three providers help us, and we’ll give you 30 days’ notice before adding another.
  • If there’s a breach that affects your data, we tell you within 48 hours.
  • You can export your data any time. When you delete your account, we delete it, and backups expire within 30 days.
  • It applies automatically. If you need a signed copy, email privacy@moduo.app.

01When this applies

This agreement applies when you, or the company or organization you act for, use Moduo and the data in your workspaces includes personal data that the GDPR or UK GDPR covers. In it, “you” means that customer, and “we” and “us” mean Ringdove sp. z o.o., which provides Moduo until Moduo’s own company exists (see Who provides Moduo).

You accept it by accepting the terms. Nothing extra to sign. If your organization needs a signed copy for its records, email privacy@moduo.app and we’ll send one with the same text.

It doesn’t cover what we do with data for our own purposes, like your account details, billing, analytics you agreed to, or the waitlist. There, we’re the controller, and our privacy policy applies.

02Who does what

  • You are the controller. You decide which personal data goes into Moduo and why. You make sure you have a legal basis for it, that you’ve told the people concerned what they need to know, and that your instructions to us are lawful.
  • We are your processor. We process that data only for you, as this agreement describes.
  • If you’re a processor yourself, for example an agency working for a client, we’re your subprocessor. You confirm that your controller has allowed this, and you pass on anything from them we need to know.

03What we process

Purpose
To provide Moduo to you and the people you share workspaces with: storing and syncing your data across devices, showing it to the people allowed to see it, sending the emails you trigger, like invites and booking confirmations, and supporting you when you ask.
What we do with it
Store, organize, sync, display, send, back up, export and delete it. Nothing else.
Whose data
Members of your workspaces, your contacts, people who book meetings with you and their guests, people in your calendar events, people in email threads you link to Moduo, and anyone else you add.
What data

Names, email addresses, phone numbers and other contact details; profile pictures; and whatever you write about people in tasks, notes, calendar events, chat messages and comments.

For booking pages: the guest’s chosen time, time zone, note and answers to your questions. For linked email threads: sender, subject, a short snippet and the date. Email messages themselves stay on your computer in the desktop app.

Sensitive data
Moduo isn’t built for health data or other special categories under Article 9 GDPR, or for data about criminal convictions. Don’t add them unless you have a legal basis and have checked our security is enough for them.
How long
For as long as you use Moduo, and then until we delete the data as Deletion at the end describes.

04Your instructions

We process your personal data only on your documented instructions. Your instructions are this agreement, the terms, and what you do in Moduo: adding, sharing, publishing, exporting or deleting things, and connecting other services. Anything more needs your agreement in writing.

We don’t use your data for our own purposes. We don’t sell it, use it for ads, or train AI models on it.

If the law makes us process it in some other way, we’ll tell you first, unless the law forbids that. If we think an instruction breaks data protection law, we’ll tell you and may refuse to follow it.

When you connect a service yourself, like Google, Microsoft, Zoom or your own AI assistant, data moves to it on your instruction. That service isn’t our subprocessor. It handles the data under its own terms with you.

05Confidentiality

Only people who need to, to run and support Moduo, can access your personal data. Today that’s Moduo’s two founders. Everyone with access is bound to keep it confidential. We look at the content of your workspaces only when you ask us to, to fix a problem, or when the law requires it.

06Security

We take the technical and organizational measures Article 32 of the GDPR requires, suited to the risk. They include:

  • encryption of all connections to Moduo, and of stored data by our database provider
  • sign-in tokens for services you connect encrypted before we store them, and API keys stored only as hashes
  • access rules in the database itself (row-level security) that limit each person to the workspaces and items they’re allowed to see
  • sign-in with one-time codes sent by email, and API keys you can limit and revoke
  • production access limited to the founders
  • backups that expire within 30 days
  • reviewing changes to sensitive parts of Moduo, like permissions and account deletion, before they go live

We may improve these measures over time, but we won’t make them weaker overall. You’re responsible for the security of your own devices, email account and API keys, and for who you invite and what you publish.

Before we add or replace a subprocessor, we’ll update this list and email you at least 30 days ahead. If you have a reasonable data protection objection, tell us at privacy@moduo.app within that time. We’ll try to find a solution. If we can’t, you can stop using Moduo and delete your account before the change.

08Transfers outside the EU

Our database is in London. The European Commission has confirmed that the UK protects personal data to EU standards, so no extra safeguards are needed there.

Supabase, Vercel and Resend are US companies, so some data may be processed in the US. Those transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, and on the EU’s standard contractual clauses in their data processing terms. We only allow a transfer outside the EU or UK when one of the safeguards in Chapter V of the GDPR covers it.

09Helping you

Requests from people

Most requests you can handle yourself in Moduo: find, correct and delete data, or export a workspace in Settings → Advanced → Export workspace data. If you need more, we’ll help. If someone asks us directly about data in your workspace, we’ll pass the request to you and won’t answer it ourselves unless you ask us to.

Assessments and authorities

We’ll give you the information you reasonably need for a data protection impact assessment, a consultation with a supervisory authority, or to show you meet your own obligations.

10Data breaches

If we become aware of a breach that affects personal data we process for you, we’ll email the workspace owner without undue delay, and within 48 hours. We’ll say what happened, what data and how many people may be affected, the likely consequences, what we’re doing about it, and who to contact. If we don’t know everything yet, we’ll send what we know and follow up.

We’ll help you meet your own duty to notify your supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where needed, the people affected. Telling you about a breach isn’t an admission of fault.

11Audits and information

We’ll give you the information you need to show that we keep to this agreement. Ask at privacy@moduo.app; we answer most questions in writing within 14 days.

If a written answer isn’t enough, or a supervisory authority asks for it, you or an independent auditor you choose can inspect how we process your data. Give us at least 30 days’ notice, keep it to normal business hours, and make sure the auditor keeps what they see confidential. Each side pays its own costs, unless the audit finds we seriously broke this agreement. We may point to our providers’ own audit reports for their systems.

12Deletion at the end

You can export your data any time. When you delete your account, or your use of Moduo ends, we delete the personal data we process for you, as our privacy policy describes. Copies in backups expire within 30 days. We keep data longer only where the law requires it.

Things you shared with other people’s workspaces stay with them, because they’re part of those workspaces. If you need something deleted from there too, tell us.

If we end the beta or close Moduo, you get at least 30 days to export your data first, as the terms describe.

13Liability and law

The liability rules in our terms apply to this agreement too, unless the GDPR doesn’t allow it. This agreement lasts as long as we process personal data for you.

For personal data, this agreement comes before the terms if they disagree. Where standard contractual clauses apply, they come before both.

Polish law applies, and disputes go to the Polish courts, unless the standard contractual clauses or data protection law say otherwise.

14Changes and contact

We may update this agreement, for example when Moduo’s company is set up or paid plans launch. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change matters for you, we’ll email you at least 30 days before it takes effect. We won’t lower the protection it gives your data.
