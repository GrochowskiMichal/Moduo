Fast.

Everything is a keystroke away. Moduo is built so you can run your day without touching the mouse.

  • ⌘KFind anything across tasks, notes, contacts, email and events.
  • ⇧⌘KCapture a thought without leaving what you’re doing.
  • ⌘NCreate something new in the module you’re in.
  • ⌘1–6Jump straight to any module.
  • JKMove through your inbox.
  • ESRArchive, snooze or reply to an email.
  • TTurn the open email into a task.
  • ?See every shortcut.

    What we won’t build.

    Saying no keeps Moduo light. These are on purpose.

    • Databases you design before you work

      You came here to work, not to model your life in tables.

    • An AI button on every screen

      Bring your own model over MCP. We don’t train AI models, so there’s nothing to train on your work.

    • A scheduler that moves your day for you

      Nothing moves unless you move it. Your plan stays yours.

    • A wall of red overdue tasks

      Falling behind is normal. Shame isn’t a feature.

    Questions.

    Anything else? Write to support@moduo.app.

    When do invites go out?

    In waves, starting soon. Join the waitlist and we’ll send you one email when it’s your turn.

    What do I get for joining early?

    The founding price: Pro at $8 a month for as long as you stay subscribed. Duo is $13 a month, and Team is $10 a seat for the first year.

    Where does email work?

    In the desktop app: Mac first, then Windows. On the web you still see follow-ups, snoozed threads and everything linked to an email.

    Which email accounts can I connect?

    Gmail, iCloud and any other provider that supports IMAP.

    Which platforms does it run on?

    Mac and web today. Windows is in progress. iPhone and Android apps are on the roadmap.

    Can I bring my notes and contacts?

    Yes. Import a Notion export or Markdown files, and contacts from a CSV.

    Does Moduo train AI on my data?

    We don’t train AI models at all. If you connect an assistant over MCP, it follows its own provider’s data rules, so limit what each key can see, or run a model on your own machine.

    Does it work offline?

    Losing your connection won’t stop you working. Your changes sync when you’re back online.

    How do plans and teams work?

    Free covers one person and every module. Pro adds more workspaces, email accounts and AI keys that can edit. Duo is for exactly two people. Team starts at three seats and adds roles, guests and admin controls. Chat and calls come with Duo and Team.

    What happens to my data if I leave?

    Export your workspace data from Settings whenever you like, and delete your account in one step.
