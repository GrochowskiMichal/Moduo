    Make it yours

    Your workspace should look like your work.
    Not ours.

    Every workspace picks its own modules and its own home screen. The studio doesn’t need your side project’s mind map — so it never sees it.

    Principles

    Things we will never build.

    Every all-in-one dies the same way: it keeps adding things. This is our list of things we won’t.

    • Databases you design before you can work.

      You came here to get things done — not to model your life in tables.

    • An AI button on every screen.

      Connect the assistant you already trust. Your workspace never trains anyone’s model.

    • A wall of red overdue tasks.

      Falling behind is normal. Shame isn’t a feature.

    Your whole working life.
    One quiet window.

    Free to start. Bring your inbox — Moduo does the linking. Leave whenever you like; your data comes with you.
