Principles

Built to stay out of the way.

An all-in-one only works if it’s light. These are the promises that keep it that way.

  • Instant

    A native app, not a heavy browser wrapper. Opening a task, searching, and switching views should feel immediate — even in a big workspace.

  • Fair

    Pay for the people you actually have. No seat minimums. A free tier students can genuinely use.

  • Yours

    Every note, task, contact, and number exports cleanly. Your data is yours — even if you leave.

  • Bring your own AI

    No assistant stapled onto every screen. Connect the AI you already use over MCP. Moduo never trains on your workspace.
