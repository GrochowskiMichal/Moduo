    Make it yours

    Your home is a board.
    Fill it with anything.

    Install the modules a workspace needs from the store, then pin, resize and recolor widgets from any of them — like the widgets on your desktop. A timer next to a budget next to today’s tasks.

    Every workspace keeps its own modules and its own board.

    Home

    Drag to rearrange Drag the corner to resize Customize ⌥⇧→Resize with the keyboard

    Store

    Adding a widget installs its module. Removing a module takes its widgets off the board.
