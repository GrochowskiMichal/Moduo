    On purpose

    What we refuse to build.

    Every all-in-one dies the same way: it keeps adding things. Here’s our list of things we won’t.

    • Databases you design before you workYou came here to do the work, not to model your life in tables.
    • An AI button on every screenBring your own model over MCP. We never train on your workspace.
    • A scheduler that moves your day for youWe suggest, with a reason. You decide.
    • A wall of red overdue tasksFalling behind is normal. Shame isn’t a feature.
