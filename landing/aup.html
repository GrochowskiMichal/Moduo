Acceptable use policy

What you can’t do with Moduo, how to report a problem, and what we do when someone breaks the rules. It’s part of our terms.

Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version

  • Use Moduo lawfully and respect other people.
  • No spam or phishing, including through booking pages, invites and published notes.
  • Don’t attack Moduo, other accounts or our providers. Found a security hole? Tell us.
  • Your AI assistant and your API keys act as you. The same rules apply to them.
  • Report illegal content or abuse to hello@moduo.app.
  • If we have to act, we start small, tell you why, and you can ask us to look again.

01What this covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses Moduo: the web and desktop apps, workspaces, published notes, booking pages, invites, and the API and AI connector. It also applies to anything acting for you, like an AI assistant or a script using your API key.

If you run a workspace, make sure the people you invite follow it too. The terms still apply in full. If something here and the terms seem to disagree, the terms win.

02Content

Don’t put anything into Moduo, or share anything through it, that:

  • is illegal, or helps someone do something illegal
  • sexually exploits or endangers children. We report it to the authorities.
  • is intimate imagery of someone shared without their consent
  • promotes terrorism or violent extremism, or threatens violence
  • attacks people for who they are, such as their race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, sexual orientation or age
  • infringes someone else’s copyright, trademark or other rights
  • contains malware, or links to it

03Other people

Moduo holds details about people who aren’t Moduo users: contacts, booking guests, email senders, meeting attendees. Treat their data with care. Don’t use Moduo to:

  • harass, bully, threaten or stalk anyone
  • publish someone’s private details without their permission
  • impersonate a person or company, or mislead people about who you are
  • collect or store personal data you have no right to, such as scraped or bought contact lists

If you hold other people’s data for your business, you’re responsible for it under data protection law. Our data processing agreement covers how we handle it for you.

04Sharing and publishing

Booking pages, workspace invites and published notes reach people outside your account, so they get extra rules. Don’t use them to:

  • send spam, or messages people didn’t ask for in bulk
  • phish, which means tricking people into giving away passwords, money or personal details
  • pretend to be Moduo or another service, for example with a fake sign-in page in a published note
  • host files or pages for other sites, like a free web host or file locker

Only invite people you know, and only to workspaces they’d expect to join.

05Security

Don’t:

  • try to get into accounts, workspaces or data that aren’t yours
  • probe, scan or test Moduo or our providers’ systems for weaknesses without our permission
  • get around sign-in, permissions, rate limits or other protections
  • share your account, or let someone else use your sign-in codes or API keys

Found a security problem?

Tell us at hello@moduo.app with the steps to reproduce it. Test only on your own account and data, don’t look at or keep other people’s data, don’t disrupt the service, and give us a fair chance to fix it before you talk about it publicly. If you do that in good faith, we won’t take action against you.

06Using the service fairly

Moduo is shared by everyone who uses it. Don’t:

  • overload or disrupt Moduo, for example with floods of requests or very large automated imports
  • scrape Moduo, or copy it to build a competing product
  • create accounts in bulk or by automated means, or sell or trade invites
  • get around plan limits, trials or the invite system
  • resell Moduo or offer it as part of your own service without our written agreement

Normal heavy use is fine. If your use starts to affect other people, we’ll get in touch before we limit anything, unless it’s urgent.

07AI assistants and API keys

You can connect your own AI assistant or scripts with an API key. Whatever they do in Moduo counts as you doing it, so this whole policy applies to them.

  • Keep your keys secret, give each one only the access it needs, and revoke any key you no longer use in Settings → API keys.
  • Check what your assistant does on your behalf, especially when it changes or shares things.
  • Don’t use the API to do anything this policy doesn’t allow in the app, or to get around its limits.

If a key looks compromised or is being misused, we may revoke it straight away and tell you.

08Reporting a problem

Anyone can report illegal content or a breach of this policy, including people who don’t use Moduo. Email hello@moduo.app and include:

  • the link to the content, like a published note or booking page, or enough detail for us to find it
  • why you think it’s illegal or breaks this policy
  • your name and email address, unless you’re reporting child sexual abuse material, which you can report anonymously
  • a statement that you believe your report is accurate and complete

We confirm we got your report, look at it carefully and without bias, and tell you what we decided. We can only see content in private workspaces when the law requires it or someone with access shows it to us.

09What we do

When something breaks this policy, we do the least that fixes it. Depending on how serious it is, we may:

  • ask you to change or remove it
  • unpublish a note or turn off a booking page
  • revoke an API key
  • limit, suspend or close the account
  • report it to the authorities, where the law requires it or someone is in danger

We tell you what we did and why, unless the law stops us or telling you would put someone at risk. Unless it’s urgent, you get time to fix the problem and to export your data first.

If you think we got it wrong, reply to our email or write to hello@moduo.app within 6 months. Someone who wasn’t involved in the first decision will look at it again. You can also go to court, as our terms describe.

10Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Moduo grows. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change matters for you, we’ll email you before it takes effect.
