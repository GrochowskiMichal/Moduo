Privacy policy

What Moduo collects, why, who helps us run it, and what you can ask of us. This covers our website at moduo.app, the waitlist, and the Moduo apps.

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

  • Analytics run only if you say yes: on the website in the cookie banner, in the app if it asks you. They never record what you type.
  • Your waitlist email is for your invite. Build updates come only if you ask for them.
  • If you use the app, we store your account and workspaces so Moduo works on every device.
  • Our database is in London, UK. Analytics stay in the EU.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or train AI models on your content.
  • You can see, export, correct or delete your data. Email privacy@moduo.app.

02The waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we store:

Your email address
To send your invite when it’s your turn. Legal basis: you asked us to (steps before a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
Spam protection details
Which form you used, your browser’s user agent, the website the form was on, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address. We never store the IP address itself. These stop spam and abuse, such as one network signing up hundreds of addresses. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a clean list (Article 6(1)(f)).
Build updates, if you ask
If you tick “Also send me build updates”, we store that choice and when you made it. Updates come about once a month. Before the first one, we’ll email you to confirm. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw any time.

We only email waitlist addresses about your invite, the updates you asked for, and important changes to this policy. Your browser also remembers that you joined, so the page can show you’re on the list (see Cookies).

03Our website

Hosting. Our website runs on Vercel. Like any web server, it receives your IP address and browser details with each request and keeps them in logs for a short time, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)).

Fonts. Our pages load their fonts from jsDelivr, a public content delivery network, so jsDelivr also sees your IP address. If you try another font in the “Make it yours” demo, that font comes from Google Fonts in the same way.

Analytics. Only if you say yes. See the next section.

04Analytics

We use PostHog to see how people use our website and the Moduo app, so we can make them better. Both run analytics only if you say yes. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we don’t ask, and analytics stay off.

On our website

Analytics run on the main page and the manifesto, and only after you click Accept in the cookie banner. When they run, PostHog records:

  • the pages you view, your clicks and scrolling, and which sections you reach
  • session recordings, which replay how you moved through the page. Everything you type is masked, and we hide your email address after you join.
  • heatmaps, clicks that don’t do anything, page errors and loading speed
  • your browser, device and screen size, and your IP address, which PostHog may use to estimate your location
  • a random ID saved in a cookie, so repeat visits can be counted

We don’t link any of this to your name or email, and we don’t build a profile of you. Before you choose, your browser downloads PostHog’s code and settings from PostHog, but nothing is recorded or stored until you accept. You can change your choice any time in Cookie settings on the main page.

In the Moduo app

After you sign in, the app may ask whether you want to share usage analytics. It asks on each device, and keeps asking until you answer. Until you say yes, it records nothing, and PostHog’s code isn’t even loaded. Your answer is saved for your account on that device.

If you say yes, the app sends PostHog:

  • a few events about how you use the app, like opening it or signing out. Never what you type or what you put into Moduo, like your tasks, notes, emails or contacts.
  • your account ID, a random code that ties these events together. Never your email address or your name.
  • which part of the app you were in, like Tasks or Notes, without any IDs, links or search terms
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and time zone, which version of the app you use and whether it’s the web or desktop app, and your IP address, which PostHog may use to estimate your location
  • random IDs for your device and session, kept in the app’s storage on your device so your visits can be counted

The app doesn’t make session recordings or heatmaps, and it doesn’t track your clicks. We use these events only to understand how Moduo is used and what to fix first. They’re kept under your account ID so we can see how use changes over time, never for ads or to make decisions about you. Turn analytics off any time in Settings → Preferences → Privacy, on each device where you said yes: that stops new events and deletes everything the app has sent under your account. PostHog removes the deleted events in its weekly clean-up. Signing out stops them until you sign in again, and clears what PostHog stored on your device.

Legal basis for both: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the Polish rules on cookies).

05The Moduo app

Moduo is in a private beta. You can only create an account with an invite.

Your account

Your email address, name, profile picture and settings. We use them to sign you in with emailed codes and to run your account. Legal basis: our contract with you, the terms (Article 6(1)(b)).

Your workspaces

Everything you put into Moduo: tasks, notes, calendar events, contacts, chat messages, and the links between them. We also store who belongs to each workspace and what they can see. We keep all this so Moduo works on all your devices and for the people you share a workspace with. Legal basis: our contract with you.

Profile pictures and workspace logos are public files, so anyone with the exact link can see them. If you publish a note, anyone with its link can read it and the pages inside it until you unpublish it.

Email

Email works in the desktop app. Messages travel straight between your email provider and your computer, and they’re stored on your computer. Your email password or sign-in token stays in your computer’s keychain. Our servers only receive:

  • the email accounts you connect: address, provider, status, signature and unread count
  • a short record of the threads you link, tag, snooze, follow up on or turn into tasks: sender, subject, a short snippet, the date, and any snooze or follow-up time

Calendars

When you connect a calendar from Google, Outlook, CalDAV or an ICS link, we copy its events into Moduo, including titles, times, descriptions and locations, so they show on all your devices. Sign-in tokens we keep on our servers are encrypted. Outlook and CalDAV passwords and tokens stay in your computer’s keychain.

Booking pages and invites

If you share a booking page, we store your available times and what guests send you (see Booking a meeting). If you invite someone to a workspace, we email them your name, the workspace name and a link to join.

Billing

Moduo is free during the beta. When you first sign in, we start a free trial at Stripe, our payment provider. Stripe receives your email address, name and account ID, and keeps your plan and trial dates. Nobody is charged during the beta. Legal basis: our contract with you.

Your AI assistant

You can connect your own AI assistant, like Claude or ChatGPT, with an API key you create in Settings → API keys. A key can only reach what you allow, and you can revoke it any time. We store a fingerprint (hash) of the key, never the key itself. What your assistant reads is then handled by its provider under its own terms. That’s your choice, so check their privacy policy.

Other services the app talks to

  • Weather widget. If you add it, your browser sends the city you search for, or its coordinates, to Open-Meteo, a free weather service.
  • Desktop updates. The desktop app checks GitHub for new versions, so GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Services you connect. When you connect Google, Microsoft, Zoom, or your own email and calendar servers, data moves between them and Moduo because you asked for it. Each handles it under its own terms.

The app has no ad trackers. It runs analytics only if you say yes (see Analytics in the app).

Google user data

If you connect a Google account, we use the data Google shares with us only to show and sync your calendar and email in Moduo, and to create meetings from your booking page. We don’t sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

06Booking a meeting

Moduo users can share a booking page at moduo.app/book. If you book a meeting through one:

  • We collect your name and email, the time you choose, your time zone, your note, your answers to the host’s questions, and the emails of any guests you add.
  • We use them to create the meeting for the host and to email you a confirmation with a link to cancel.
  • If the host uses Google Calendar, the meeting goes into their Google calendar, and Google sends you and your guests an invitation. If the host uses Zoom, we create a Zoom meeting on their account with your details in its description.
  • You’re added as a contact in the host’s workspace.

We do this on the host’s behalf, and the host decides what happens with your details afterwards. To change or delete them, ask the host. You can also write to us and we’ll help.

10Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access)
  • correct it (rectification)
  • delete it (erasure)
  • limit how we use it (restriction)
  • send it to you in a machine-readable file (portability)
  • stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interest (objection)

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it any time. That doesn’t affect what we did before.

To use your rights, email privacy@moduo.app, ideally from the address you use with Moduo. It’s free. We answer within one month, and we may ask you to confirm it’s you.

You can also do some of this yourself. Export a workspace in Settings → Advanced → Export workspace data. Delete your account in Settings → Account.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Polish supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl. You can also complain to the authority where you live or work. We’d like the chance to fix it first, so please tell us too.

You don’t have to give us any data. Without an email address, though, we can’t send you an invite or create your account. We don’t make decisions about you based only on automated processing.

11Cookies

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. Here is everything ours save. We don’t use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

On moduo.app

moduo:consent
Local storage. Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics, so we don’t ask again. Stays until you clear your browser data.
moduo:waitlist
Local storage, saved when you join. Remembers your email so the page can show you’re on the list. “Use a different email” removes it.
moduo:waitlist-updates
Local storage. Remembers your build updates choice.
moduo-manifesto-theme, moduo-manifesto-motion
Local storage. Remember light or dark, and motion, on our text pages. Saved only if you change them.
PostHog

Only after you accept. A cookie called ph_…_posthog (the middle part is our project key), the same data in local storage, and ph_… entries in session storage. They hold a random ID and session details, so visits can be counted and recordings pieced together. The cookie lasts up to a year.

If you decline, PostHog saves that choice in local storage (__ph_opt_in_out_…) so it stays off.

In the Moduo app

The web app uses no cookies. It keeps your sign-in session, your settings and a copy of your notes in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB, so you stay signed in and pages load fast. Signing out ends the session. The desktop app keeps its data on your computer and in its keychain.

moduo:consent:<your account ID>
Local storage. Remembers whether you said yes or no to analytics in the app, for your account on that device, so we don’t ask again.
PostHog
Only if you say yes. ph_moduo_app in local and session storage, ph_moduo_app_… entries in session storage, and __ph_opt_in_out_moduo_app in local storage. They hold your account ID, random IDs for your device and session, and a note that analytics are on. They also hold, on your device only and never sent, the address of the page where your session started (with sign-in and invite codes hidden), the address of the page that led there, and, if you came from a search engine, the words you searched for. All of it is cleared when you turn analytics off or sign out. No cookies.

The desktop app keeps the same entries in its own storage on your computer.

Your choices

On the website, change your analytics choice any time in Cookie settings on the main page. In the app, use Settings → Preferences → Privacy. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser’s settings.

12Security

Connections to Moduo are encrypted, and our database provider encrypts stored data. Tokens for services you connect are encrypted before we store them, and API keys are stored only as hashes. If a data breach puts your rights at risk, we’ll tell you and UODO as the law requires.
