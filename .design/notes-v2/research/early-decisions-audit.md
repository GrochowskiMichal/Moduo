## 0. Headline

Notes' big shapes are right and should survive: **everything is a page** (nesting to any depth), **the CRDT document with markdown at every door**, **offline writing**, **"a checkbox stays a checkbox; a task in a note is the real task"**, **publish-to-web**, and **agents as authors**. What has to change is everything that assumed **one writer in a two-person workspace**: the Inbox is derived from the tree's shape and shared with everyone, pins are a workspace flag, the welcome note is per workspace, the editor speaks its own grammar (`@` people only, `/contact` nouns), and the sidebar shows the writer's housekeeping (Published, Archive, Trash) to every reader.

Three facts the July plan didn't have now bind it:
1. **Per-note sharing already exists.** The October permissions work gave notes private/shared access with inheritance, in the database and in a Share menu ([perm_sharing.sql:293-296, 590-615](../../../supabase/migrations/20261006210000_perm_sharing.sql); [notes-page.tsx:661-672](../../../src/routes/pages/notes-page.tsx)). The July docs still say "deferred".
2. **Tasks v3 decided the app-wide rules** Notes must adopt: the `@ # /` grammar, References, the panel dropdown, the capture registry, a personal Inbox, personal pins, templates, one notification table, per-person onboarding.
3. **The hackathon makes the reader the main user.** No early decision speaks for someone who didn't write the notes and has never seen Moduo. That gap, more than any single decision, is what the re-plan must fill.

## 1. Root cause

### 1.1 Who Notes was first specified for

- **One capturer.** The job is quoted from a user: "Is there anybody else who thinks Notion is too complicated for just writing down ideas?" ([BRIEF.md:10](../../notes/BRIEF.md)). Capture is "open, type, done"; filing is optional, later.
- **One writer and his agents.** The ratified delta adds "AI-as-author… Agents build whole doc trees (briefs, rules, project docs)" ([DESIGN_BRIEF.md:14](../../notes/DESIGN_BRIEF.md)); Archive is for "old client work, MTG decks" (:16).
- **In a workspace of two.** "Per-user pins would need a new table; acceptable at ≤2-person alpha" ([DESIGN_BRIEF.md:123](../../notes/DESIGN_BRIEF.md)); multiplayer is "enough to build Moduo with a partner" ([data-layers.md:152](../../../docs/data-layers.md)); presence names come from the email's local part because members had no names yet ([decisions/notes.md:17](../../../docs/decisions/notes.md) NO-6 b).

The whole module was planned on 2026-07-03 and built in two days (NO-1…NO-10, 2026-07-03 → 07-04; [BUILD_LOG.md:64-80](../../../specs/BUILD_LOG.md)). `git log -- src/features/notes` shows no Notes product round since: every later change came from outside the module (NOTE-FIX-1 and IM-1 when real imports came back blank or empty, Lexical 0.49, PERM-2b…8 sharing, then Tasks v3's SH-1, TV-U3, DS-6, TV-D8/D9 touching shared files).

### 1.2 The one shape behind the reversals

Tasks' root cause was "a solo-planner assumption stored as a column". Calendar's was "a solo planner's habit stored as the data model". **Notes' is: a solo writer's habits stored as the workspace's shared structure.** Things that belong to one person (what I haven't filed yet, what I pinned, whether I've seen the welcome, when my trash empties) were stored as the shape of the tree or as flags everyone shares:

| What Notes stores | The solo-writer assumption | What reverses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inbox = any root note with no children**, derived from the tree, no owner ([tree.ts:4-16, 101-113](../../../src/features/notes/tree.ts); [decisions/notes.md:23](../../../docs/decisions/notes.md) (a), flagged then: "a deliberately standalone root note lives in Inbox forever") | Every loose top-level page is *my* unfiled thought | Tasks 20: the Inbox is personal ("people capture freely only when it's private"). And in a workspace with more than one member, every new root note gets a workspace grant at the default level, Can edit ([perm_sharing.sql:44, 600-615](../../../supabase/migrations/20261006210000_perm_sharing.sql)), so a ⌘⇧N half-thought is posted to the whole team's "Inbox". A hackathon "Rules" page with no sub-pages sits in every participant's Inbox, not in the tree |
| **Pins = `is_pinned` on the note**, workspace-wide ([DESIGN_BRIEF.md:123](../../notes/DESIGN_BRIEF.md); spec A12) | Two people agree on what's pinned | Tasks U6-6: pins follow the person, in synced preferences ([decisions/tasks.md:184](../../../docs/decisions/tasks.md)); the "new table" excuse is gone |
| **Welcome note seeded once per workspace**, flag in one device's local storage, skipped if any note exists ([use-notes-module.ts:216-247](../../../src/features/notes/hooks/use-notes-module.ts)) | The first person to open Notes is the only one to teach | Tasks 34: once per person per module; teammates who join later get their own |
| **Trash empties when someone opens Notes** (client sweep, spec A13) | The writer opens Notes daily | AT-1's server clock (`purge-deleted`) empties tasks, projects and files but not notes ([purge-deleted/index.ts:2-15](../../../supabase/functions/purge-deleted/index.ts)): two trash clocks |
| **Sidebar rows Published · Archive · Trash, always there** ([DESIGN_BRIEF.md:47](../../notes/DESIGN_BRIEF.md)) | The reader is the curator | Tasks 98 + 29e: no permanent housekeeping rows; groups show only when they hold something |
| **A child's page-row is written only by the writer's open editor** ([decisions/notes.md:21](../../../docs/decisions/notes.md) NO-4 c) | Children are made by hand, one at a time, while the parent is open | Imports and agents build whole trees ([DESIGN_BRIEF.md:14](../../notes/DESIGN_BRIEF.md)); their parents show no list of their children |
| **A task made in a note lands in "the Inbox bucket in Tasks"** ([DESIGN_BRIEF.md:79](../../notes/DESIGN_BRIEF.md)) | The note's writer will do the task | Tasks 20 made that Inbox private: a deliverable an organiser writes into a team note lands where only the organiser sees it |

### 1.3 A second, smaller cluster: local rules ratified before the app-wide ones existed

Not a solo assumption, and it shouldn't be forced into the one above. In July Notes was the first module with an editor, so it ratified its own conventions; Tasks v3 has since ratified app-wide ones, and they disagree: the `@`/`/` grammar, the slash nouns, the task line as its own node, the panel's tabs and the "Detail" label, Notes' own Realtime feed and device cache, its own comment composer settings, and "Trash" as a word. Each is a row below. None needs a data reversal; each needs adoption.

### 1.4 What the root cause hides: the reader

Every July decision is about writing (capture, grammar, sync, markdown in, AI writing). Nothing decides how a person who didn't write the notes finds and follows them. For the hackathon that person is the main user. Concretely today: no "start here" page; standalone pages hide in Inbox; AI-built parents don't list their children; nothing marks what changed since you last read; and **a note's markdown degrades once it's edited** (the save path writes a lossy copy that drops links, bold and tables, and that copy feeds the published page, export and MCP: [gotchas/notes.md:34](../../../docs/gotchas/notes.md)). So an organiser fixing a typo in the rules would strip the links from the public rules page.

## 5. Open product questions (for the designer, each with a recommendation)

Numbered for this file only; the re-plan's own calls (N1, N2, …) get numbered when round 1 is written.

1. **Is a quick note private until you file it, like a task in your Inbox?** Today a note you capture with ⌘⇧N is instantly visible to the whole workspace, in a section called "Inbox". *Recommend: yes.* Captured notes land in your own Inbox, only you see them, and filing one (moving it under a page, or "Move to workspace") shares it the usual way. Pages made inside the tree are the team's from the start. A standalone page like "Rules" sits in the tree, never in an Inbox.

2. **Pins: personal, plus one "Start here" page for the workspace?** *Recommend: yes.* Pins become yours (as in Tasks). Separately, an owner or organiser can mark one page as the workspace's "Start here": it opens first for anyone who hasn't opened Notes before and stays at the top of the tree. This is the hackathon's front door, and it doubles as the welcome in a prepared workspace.

3. **Should `/` still create a contact or company from a note** (`/contact Anna` → new contact), or only tasks and pages, as Tasks decided for now? *Recommend:* `/` creates pages, tasks and contacts (a founder taking meeting notes meets new people); linking anything that exists moves to `@`. One rule: `@` links what exists, `/` makes something new.

4. **Does `#launch` in a note's text tag the note?** Tasks says that in prose `#` only links. *Recommend: yes for notes*: typing a tag in a note's body also tags the note (the Bear/Obsidian habit, and how students and creators already work), and removing the last one untags it. In task descriptions and comments `#` keeps linking only.

5. **What does a task look like inside a note: a checklist line or the task card?** *Recommend: the checklist line*, built from the shared parts (the status icon of its category, the due date in the app's date words, "Private item" when you can't see it), with the title editable in place. "Show as" offers Line · Card, so a deliverables list can show cards. This keeps Notes' best moment and Tasks' one chip language.

6. **Where does a task you make in a note go?** *Recommend:* into the project the note (or its parent page) is linked to; otherwise into your Inbox. It's always linked back "From: <note>", and the line shows its project. An organiser's "Deliverables" page linked to the team project files tasks where the team sees them.

7. **Bring back folding?** *Recommend: collapsible headings*, not toggle blocks. Any heading can fold the section under it; it's a reading aid, so exports and imports stay plain markdown. FAQs and long rules read as a list of questions.

8. **How does a page show it's private?** *Recommend:* a small lock after the name of private pages in the tree, and nothing on shared ones (most pages in a team are shared). Privacy stays per page, and sub-pages follow their parent.

9. **Comments: anchor them to the text?** *Recommend: not for the hackathon cut.* Keep quote-comments (select text → Comment), and move Notes onto the shared comment feed now: `@` people and things, `/today`, the six reactions, edit and delete. Text-anchored highlights can come later if reviewing briefs together proves common.

10. **How do readers learn a page changed?** *Recommend:* a quiet "Updated" mark on pages changed since you last opened them, and no notification for edits. Announcing a change to everyone is a Chat message (the Chat boundary), not a Notes feature.

11. **One door for a new note?** *Recommend:* ⌘⇧K then ⌘2 is the quick note; ⌘⇧N opens that same capture already set to Note (one door, two keys); ⌘N inside Notes makes a page where you are.

12. **Live cursors?** *Recommend: no.* Keep the "who's here" avatars; cursors add noise for a team of five and aren't needed for co-writing a pitch.

13. **Templates for notes?** *Recommend: yes, Tasks' minimal version*: "Save as template" on a page (with its sub-pages), "From template…" in New and in capture, a list in Settings to rename or delete, no gallery, never asked when you create a page. Plus "Duplicate with sub-pages".

14. **The Archive, Recently deleted and Published rows?** *Recommend:* off the sidebar, into its ⋯ (as Tasks 98); "Trash" is called "Recently deleted" everywhere; a published page carries a small "Published" mark in the tree.

15. **Mindmaps in notes?** *Recommend:* remove `/mindmap`; existing embeds stay visible, read-only, until the Mindmap has its own plan.
