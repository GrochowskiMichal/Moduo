What blind critics make of **today's Calendar**: Mike's rethink as built, from origin/maciej @ e566e71e. This is the yardstick for the round-1b prototype and, later, for the rebuild.

- **How it was measured.** `/product-studio criticise` ran in two steps.

  - **The dossier** is in the gitignored `.lab/20261123-1208-calendar-baseline/`.

## What every critic read as the idea

All three agree: "the week and the to-do list are one planning surface": Plan beside the grid with Find time, due counts on the day headers, tasks dropped onto the grid as chips, and the typed box that finds a time when a teammate is free too. Its signature moment is "Ana Ruiz is free", followed by a hop to time you're both free.

All three also say the grid itself is anonymous: "take the Plan panel away and it could be any calendar". That is what holds character at 7.

## What's strong (keep)

These were named by at least two runs.
- **Undo instead of confirm** for delete, duplicate, moving one repeat, placing a task, and removing an account (with an inline explanation).
- **Designed states:**
  - **Empty:** "Your week is open", with ⌘N, Connect Google and T/D/W.
  - **Error:** "events may be missing. Tasks are current."
  - **Also:** offline and reconnect.
  - **The events skeleton.**
- **The typed new-event box:** inline chips, "first free", an honest "No invite sent: connect Google".
- **Speed:** key to paint 24 ms, click 40 ms, view switch 126 ms (p95).
- **Focus:** all 25 focus stops are visible, and tooltips teach shortcuts.
- **Keyboard journeys at the shortest path:** jump, quick create, compose with a teammate, copy-paste.

**Candidates** (one run only, kept for the build's checklist):
- Past events lose their outline and read as labels.
- The mini-month jump doesn't mark the picked day.
- An empty workspace shows its only calendar unticked.
- Long Plan titles aren't clamped, and URLs break mid-word.
- The right-click menu shows no shortcuts.
- "Next free time" leaves the morning that was typed.
- The events skeleton has an all-day row the loaded week lacks, so the grid jumps.
- A stray "⇧A" in the all-day gutter.
- "Remove this account…" isn't aligned with anything.

**From the code, not the critics:** the holes the critics *can't* see are the ones §1.0 already lists. Calendars refresh only while a Calendar page is open (C30); meetings older than 30 days are deleted (C30); "Took longer" logs planned time (C3); placing a task moves its one slot (C22); there are no alerts (C13).

## What this evidence can't show

- Native menus and window chrome (a WebKit stand-in ran).
- How drags feel.
- Save and sync latency, and rollback after a failed save.
- The guest's booking page.
- The states the rig couldn't open: bookingLink, syncError, share, previewAs, offering.
- The light theme.
- How the module is learned over weeks.

## What it changes in the plan

**No recommendation in C1–C32 changes.**
- **Confirmations.** The baseline confirms C24 (label the due count), C26 (links up front in Details) and the round-1b consistency pass (one fault line).
- **Build-phase fixes.** The rest are build-phase fixes that need no product call: H1–H7, H9–H11, H13, H15, H16.
- **Spec gate.** They go into the spec's acceptance criteria, and the lab's out-of-date journeys get updated in the first build wave so the next score isn't capped by the rig.
