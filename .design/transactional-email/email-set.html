The copy and the look of each email, ratified on 8 October 2026. The example people are made up (Tom books Anna, Anna invites Tom). Nothing here is built yet; the build plan is specs/transactional-email.md.

Who it's from

Two addresses

  • hello@moduo.app for everything about your account, invites, bookings and trial. Replies reach you both.
  • updates@news.moduo.app for the monthly build updates only, so spam complaints about a newsletter can never slow down sign-in codes.
The shell

  • The drawn Moduo lockup top left, as one image from the brand pipeline (typed here only as a stand-in), or the host's face on booking emails, like the booking page.
  • One heading, short paragraphs, at most one button.
  • Booking emails reuse the booking page's sentence, with the fixed words underlined.
  • Every email has a plain-text twin.

Comes with it

Changes inside the app

  • Sign-in screen"Resend code" shows a 60-second countdown instead of Supabase's raw error.
  • Settings → Notifications → EmailThree switches: Bookings (on), Trial and billing reminders (on), Build updates (off).
  • Each booking linkA "Remind guests" setting: Off, 1 hour before or 24 hours before. New links start at 24 hours.
  • Host bell notificationsNew booking and guest canceled, alongside the emails.
  • Booking pageStops promising a calendar invite on Zoom-only links.
  • OnboardingSays 14-day trial, not 7.
  • Privacy policyNames Resend as the sender of sign-in emails, which Supabase sends today.
