## #329 [TV-U4] Drag and drop

### 1. What it does

- **Size and checks:** 17 files, +1,539 / −323, draft, **no migrations**. Static checks are green. The Vercel failure is the Hobby build-rate limit, not a build error. The PR body reports `verify` green (2,604 tests) and a live check in a temporary harness.
- **Pure core:**
  - `dnd/drop-mode.ts` (+284 lines of tests): reorder vs nest from the raw pointer (a 40 px split); a sorted order refuses a reorder; a cross-group drop writes the group's field. Covers U4-1–3.
  - `dnd/rail-drop.ts` (+171 lines of tests): `rail:*` targets resolved through a prefix-filtered `pointerWithin`. A bucket moves the task and its subtasks; Queue queues it; My tasks assigns it to me. Covers U4-4.
- **Wiring:**
  - List rows become drag sources, hit-tested with `elementsFromPoint` (`task-list-view.tsx:597`).
  - `moveTaskToBucket` is added at `use-tasks-module.ts:1267`.
  - The rail gets `RailDropRow`, and the page's one `DndContext` handles rail drops (`tasks-plan-view.tsx:470`).
  - Board bucket-column drops now carry the subtasks too.
- **Visuals:** DS-4's drag visuals are adopted on the List, Board and rail (U4-5). Dead nest code is removed (`pointerFirstCollision`, `onto-task`).
- **Completeness: TV-U4 only.** The PR tag says U4/U5/DS-5, but **U5 and DS-5 have no code here.** Validator round 1 left these open:
  - **MAJOR:** `alreadyThere` (`drop-mode.ts:222`) loses a bucket-group drop's position.
  - **MINOR:** a nest across buckets leaves the child in its old bucket.
  - **MINOR:** the parent change and the status change go as two concurrent writes.
  - **MINOR:** the "Moved to X" toast shows even when the move failed.
  - **MINOR:** archived subtasks don't follow their parent.
  - **MINOR:** the gotchas doc is stale, and screen readers hear raw ids.
