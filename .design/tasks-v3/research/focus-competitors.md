# Focus: what competitors do

Research for the Focus redesign, 10 October 2026. Sources are official docs, changelogs and product pages unless noted. **Unverified** means seen only in a search snippet or third-party page.

## 1. Timer controls

- **Sunsama.** Space starts and stops the timer; there is a Break control ([breaks](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/breaks)). There is no End button: Esc or another view leaves Focus mode, and a running timer stays in a floating **focus bar** with play, pause and complete side by side ([Focus mode](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/focus-mode/), [focus bar](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/focus-bar/)). The timer counts up; cards show actual next to planned time ([planned and actual](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/planned-and-actual-times)); optional sounds play 10, 5 or 0 minutes before the planned time.
- **Structured.** A full-screen countdown to the end of the time block; tapping the time hides the seconds. To end, you tap an **x in the top-left corner**, which asks whether to "stop the timer" or "just minimize it" ([help](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/331010)).
- **Session (Mac).** Stop asks you to choose "Finish Session"; Siri has a separate "Abandon Session". The timer counts down, then "overflow" keeps it running, and sessions under 25 minutes are still saved ([guide](https://stayinsession.com/learn/getting-started-with-session-pomodoro-app)).
- **Tiimo.** A visual ring with Pause/Play and +1 minute; to finish early you **drag the timer to the end** ([FAQ](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq/focus-timer)).
- **Llama Life.** A countdown per task (±5 min). At zero an Overtime counter counts up, marked by "a little red dot", and you choose Add or Delete for the extra time ([overtime](https://intercom.help/llama-life/en/articles/6575141-using-the-overtime-feature), [timers](https://intercom.help/llama-life/en/articles/6456401-using-timers)).
- **Amazing Marvin.** P or a bottom-right button pauses. An optional bar fills toward the estimate, then "turns orange" with "+5m over" ([Super Focus Mode](https://help.amazingmarvin.com/en/articles/2237484-super-focus-mode-sfm)). The timer also shows in the menu bar and a floating window ([time tracking](https://amazingmarvin.com/features/time-tracking)).
- **Akiflow.** A pomodoro timer in the header plus a floating widget with skip, stop and hide; no pause documented ([Focus Timer](https://akiflow.notion.site/Focus-Timer-19253c98818480e3a441c933310b3623)).
- **TickTick.** Pomo counts down, Stopwatch counts up; pause is capped at three per Pomo "to stay in the Pomo mood" ([blog](https://blog.ticktick.com/2020/05/06/brand-new-focus-experience-ticktick/)). ± buttons sit beside the time, and the duration can be edited afterwards ([help](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782010496745472)).

**Pattern.**
- Pause always lives on the timer itself.
- Accidental ends are prevented three ways: no End at all, so leaving is harmless (Sunsama); End behind a menu (Session); End in the far corner with a stop-or-minimise choice (Structured).
- No product I found puts a bare End next to Pause, and none documents an undo.
- Time is usually a hybrid: count down to the estimate, then up. Overruns get a colour (red dot, orange bar): copy the structure, not the colour.

## 2. Untimed focus

- **Akiflow.** Focus mode (F) has no timer; the Focus Timer is separate ([Focus mode](https://akiflow.notion.site/Focus-mode-15953c988184801ab883e30e69e3ebd8)).
- **Sunsama.** Focus mode works without a running timer, and a setting counts planned time as actual if you log none ([planned and actual](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/planned-and-actual-times)).
- **Marvin.** "Show session timer" and "Show progress bar" are separate toggles, and automatic time tracking starts when you enter Focus mode: hidden timer, time still recorded ([SFM](https://help.amazingmarvin.com/en/articles/2237484-super-focus-mode-sfm)).
- **Rize** records focus, meeting and break sessions from activity and calendar, with no timer ([docs](https://docs.rize.io/sessions/overview)). **TickTick**'s Stopwatch is for sessions "without a time limit" ([Timer](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055781980423585792)).

**Pattern.** Without a timer, the list shows progress: Sunsama moves to the next task and ends on "Done for the day"; Llama Life shows the list's total and end times at the top ([help](https://intercom.help/llama-life/en/articles/6459428-can-i-see-what-time-i-ll-be-finishing-my-tasks)). Silent recording is common.

## 3. Subtasks, notes and links

- **Sunsama.** Focus mode shows notes, subtasks and comments, all editable; subtasks can be timed ([Focus mode](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/focus-mode/), [task basics](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/task-basics)).
- **Structured.** Subtasks and notes sit on the full-screen timer; add or check them off as you go ([help](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/331010)).
- **Tiimo.** Ring, countdown clock and checklist share one screen ([product page](https://www.tiimoapp.com/product/focus)).
- **Akiflow.** Edits save automatically; an "i" icon opens the task's details ([Focus mode](https://akiflow.notion.site/Focus-mode-15953c988184801ab883e30e69e3ebd8)).
- **Marvin.** With "Show subtasks one at a time" on, "you only see the next step"; projects also show their tasks one by one ([SFM](https://help.amazingmarvin.com/en/articles/2237484-super-focus-mode-sfm)).
- **Llama Life.** "Break it down" replaces a task with separate timed steps ([help](https://intercom.help/llama-life/en/articles/8332767-how-to-use-our-break-it-down-feature)).
- **Todoist** (no Focus mode). A sub-task opens in the same task view, with its parent shown above its name ([task view](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-the-task-view-to-manage-tasks-in-todoist-eDeRDO0C)).

**Pattern.** Subtasks get the main body of the focus screen, but as thin checklist rows. I found no product that opens a subtask's own notes or links inside focus. Moduo's subtasks are full tasks, so that gap is Moduo's to fill; the building blocks are Marvin's one step at a time and Todoist's parent link.

## 4. Calendar awareness

- **Sunsama.** Hover the right edge "to reveal your calendar, so you don't miss your next meeting" ([Focus mode](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/focus-mode/)).
- **Akiflow.** The same right-edge calendar, plus "your next task or event" at the **bottom** ([Focus mode](https://akiflow.notion.site/Focus-mode-15953c988184801ab883e30e69e3ebd8)).
- **Llama Life.** A Time Divider you drag into the list; only tasks above it count toward the end time ([help](https://intercom.help/llama-life/en/articles/8974299-using-the-time-divider)).
- **Things, Superlist.** Events inside Today ([Things](https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/2803583/), [Superlist](https://www.superlist.com/updates/see-your-calendar-meetings-in-today)).
- **Notion Calendar.** The menu bar previews the next event, and you choose "how much in advance" ([settings](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-calendar-settings)). **Amie**'s menu bar lists today's events ([docs](https://amie.so/documentation/desktop/menubar)).
- **Timeline apps** (Structured, Tiimo, Motion): the calendar is the list. Session and Structured start focus from an event or block.
- **Counter-signal.** A Sunsama request (6 votes) asks for timer sounds to go quiet in meetings and screen shares ([roadmap](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/improvements/p/do-not-disturb-mute-timer-focus-bar-and-pomodoro-sounds-when-in-a-meeting-or-scr)).

**Pattern.** The next event is never a headline above the task. It sits behind the screen edge, at the bottom, inside the list, or in the menu bar with a lead time you set.

## 5. Naming

- **Dated lists:** **Today** (Todoist, Things, Sunsama, Akiflow, Superlist, TickTick's "Today" and "Next 7 days", [FAQ](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055792921664028672)); **My Day** (Microsoft To Do, which "resets every night", [support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/ToDo/my-day-and-suggestions)).
- **Dateless list:** Llama Life's **Now list** and **Not-Now list** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adhd-organizer-llama-life/id6454469750)).
- **Doing surface:** **Focus** in six products (Sunsama, Akiflow, Marvin, Structured, Tiimo's Focus tab, and TickTick, which renamed "Pomo" to "Focus" in 2020); **Session** in Session and Rize; **Focus sessions** in Windows. Sunsama's single "Focus" nav item opens Focus mode on the running task or the top task.
- **Upcoming** means dated future items in Todoist and Things ([MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/todoist-foundations-key-refinements-for-the-popular-task-manager/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Things_(software))), which supports the worry that "Queue" reads like "Upcoming".
- Nobody uses **Queue**, **Up next** or **Line-up** for the personal list. **I found no evidence on which names are clearest;** the only signal is convergence.

## 6. Reach

- **Opt-in.** TickTick: Pomodoro is switched on in Settings › Features, and its tab can be removed ([help](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7055782010496745472), [tab bar](https://blog.ticktick.com/2018/10/31/ticktickcustomtabbar/)). Marvin: Super Focus Mode is a "strategy" you switch on ([strategies](https://help.amazingmarvin.com/en/articles/1951470-how-to-turn-features-on-and-off-with-strategies)).
- **Visible but optional.** Sunsama keeps Focus in the nav and F on any task, plus a setting so a timer doesn't open Focus mode, offered to a user whose Today is "mission control" ([roadmap](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/improvements/p/keep-main-window-in-today-view-show-mini-timer)).
- **Absent.** Todoist and Things have no Focus mode; Todoist leaves it to integrations such as Llama Life ([integration](https://todoist.com/integrations/apps/llama-life)), and its default home view is Today ([help](https://todoist.com/help/articles/360000281429)).
- **Long tasks.** Sunsama rolls a task over with its logged time as a "breadcrumb", or suggests subtasks or weekly objectives ([multi-day](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/pro-tips/multi-day-tasks)). Motion splits a part-logged task into "1/2" and "2/2" ([docs](https://www.usemotion.com/help/project-management/task/reference-tasks/task-states-and-task-types.md)). Tiimo keeps all-day tasks out of its Focus tab ([FAQ](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq/focus-timer)).
- **Time-blocked calendars.** Akiflow's timer picks the first task scheduled on the calendar; Tiimo's starts when a scheduled task begins; Structured and Session start from a block or event.

**Who doesn't use focus modes.** I found no direct evidence (B9 in `.design/tasks-dogfood/research-2026-10/adhd-productivity-evidence.md` says the same). Indirect signals: people who live in their list view (Sunsama's "mission control" user), meeting-heavy days (the 6-vote request), people who hyperfocus and want a hard stop instead ([roadmap](https://roadmap.sunsama.com/improvements/p/in-focus-mode-set-a-block-state-or-trigger-an-annoying-alarm-when-a-planned-task)), and all-day work (Tiimo).

## 7. Between tasks

- **Moves on quietly.** Sunsama shows the next task, then "Done for the day". In Marvin "The next task automatically appears"; encouraging cheers are an opt-in setting. Akiflow shows the next scheduled task.
- **Optional momentum.** Llama Life can start the next timer automatically ([help](https://intercom.help/llama-life/en/articles/7051604-using-the-auto-start-next-task-feature)) and shows confetti (whether it can be turned off: unverified).
- **Pause and reflect.** Session turns the background the category colour when time is up, then offers a break and notes. Structured doesn't carry focus to the next block.
- **Gamified (avoid).** Windows Focus sessions: "not break your streak" ([Windows blog](http://blogs.windows.com/windows-insider/2021/08/19/new-clock-app-for-windows-11-with-focus-sessions-rolling-out-to-windows-insiders/)). Forest: trees wither if you leave ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_(application))). Sukha: "streaks and points" ([site](https://www.thesukha.co/)). Sukha is the renamed Centered, not discontinued: centered.app redirects to thesukha.co.

**Pattern.** Calm products move straight to the next task with no screen in between and keep the recap for the end.

**Could not verify:** how you end a session in TickTick; Motion's "focus banner" (search snippet only; its help page redirected); whether Llama Life's confetti can be turned off; whether Sunsama subtasks have their own notes; any Focus mode in Amie or Superlist (none documented).

## Recommendations for Moduo

1. **Too much around the task.** Collapse the two lines above the card into one quiet row: status at one end (rec 6), a ⋯ menu with Arrange and End at the other. Show the next event once, at the bottom (Akiflow), only within a lead time the user sets (Notion Calendar); optionally let people drag a time divider into Up next (Llama Life). Drop "2 quick tasks fit": the system suggests it on its own. Move "along the way" to the project divider in Up next, and "2 done · 1h 12m" to the end-of-run summary.
2. **Pause next to End.** Make the timer the pause control (click it, or Space). Leaving Focus never ends the run (Sunsama). End lives only inside the ⋯ menu at the opposite end of the row from the timer: a menu step (Session) plus distance (Structured), with undo instead of a confirmation dialog.
3. **Name.** Use **Focus** for the nav item and the list: most competitors call the doing surface Focus, and none says Queue. Keep "Today" and "Upcoming" free for dated views. Inside, use plain verbs: Start, Pause, Done.
4. **Visible, not pushed.** Keep Focus in the nav with a one-line empty state and "Focus on this" (F) on any task; no nudges. The list must be useful without ever pressing Start. Add "Stop for today", which keeps the task on top with its time (Sunsama rollover, Motion 1/2), and let people start Focus from a calendar block.
5. **Subtasks.** Give the card's body to subtasks: each row expands in place to show its description, links and files. Offer one step at a time (Marvin): the current subtask becomes the card, with its parent linked above (Todoist). Collapse Up next while a subtask is open.
6. **"Running" and the timer.** One status element, not two. By default show no timer, record time silently and show "2 of 7" (Marvin's separate toggles). A setting offers timer off, elapsed or remaining; when on, the timer replaces "Running" and is the pause control. Overruns show a neutral "+5m", never red or orange.
