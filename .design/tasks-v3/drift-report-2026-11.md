# Tasks v3: where the shipped UI drifts from the approved prototypes

> **Date:** 2026-11-04 (an unattended overnight session; the designer was asleep, so every call below is a recommendation, not a decision).
> **Compared:** `origin/develop` @ `70053ba` (v1.7.1), the code under `apps/app/src`, against `.design/tasks-v3/prototypes/` (structure, round-1c, round-2a, round-2b: 28 frames plus their variants) and `REPLAN.md` §8.
> **Later decisions read first:** `docs/decisions/tasks.md` (every "Frames matched…" / dogfood MOD-xxx entry), `docs/decisions/design-system.md`, `specs/tasks-v3.md`, `.design/tasks-dogfood/`, `.design/tasks-polish/` and `.claude/rules/tasks-ui.md`.
> **Scope:** a report only. No app code was changed.

## Verdict

**The skeleton is right; the skin and a handful of defaults drifted.**

What holds:
- The founding 3×3 holds everywhere.
- The right panel switches only through its title-row dropdown (72/72a).
- Panel toggles live only in the bottom bar.
- Call 96's top bar and the bottom bar's Search · Capture · New shipped exactly.
- The status-category model, the Board's folded Backlog, the Timeline's bars and points, Focus's words and menus, and the Inbox triage keys all match.
- Most of what looks different at first glance is a later approved call, and is not drift (about 26 of 111 differences). Examples: tag chips with a dot, the 8 px panel gap and 16 px corners, "4 tasks ▾", the Archived footer, the timer in the top bar, Details instead of In flight in Focus.

What a designer will feel is different:
- Defaults that changed the frames' composition:
  - the right panel opens at its 280 px floor instead of 340, so the narrow layout became the norm;
  - Focus sits in a 672 px column;
  - opening a project selects its first task instead of showing Project;
  - the Board hides Done.
- A missing piece of a project's identity: no meta line under the title, and no colour square.
- Dozens of one-step size and tone slips, mostly type a size off and padding a step tighter. No single slip is big, but together they make Tasks read denser and greyer than the frames.

About **62 differences are DRIFT** (nothing approved them; most are S fixes), **26 are LATER CALLS** and **22 need the designer (UNCLEAR)**. The root cause is the one the brief names. Builders matched the REPLAN's *words* and the research tables, then filled in the visual detail from the existing primitives and tokens. Nothing checked them against the frames. Where an agent's choice "deferred to by Maciej" overrode a frame, it was recorded; where a primitive's default quietly overrode it, it wasn't.

## How to read this

- **DRIFT:** nothing approved the shipped form: no REPLAN call, spec line or later decision. The default answer is "match the frame".
- **LATER CALL:** approved after the frame; the source is cited. *(designer)* means a "Who: Maciej" entry; *(agent)* means an "— the agent's choices (deferred to by Maciej)" entry, which he may still want to see.
- **UNCLEAR:** two sources disagree, or the frame is ambiguous; the designer has to say.
- **Notice:** how much the designer would notice in daily use. **Fix:** S is under an hour, M is a block-sized change, L is a spec.
- Paths are relative to `apps/app/src/` unless they start with `docs/`, `specs/` or `.claude/`. Inside a section, `ui/…`, `capture/…` and bare Tasks file names are under `features/tasks/`, and `tasks.md:NNN` means `docs/decisions/tasks.md`. Line numbers are from `70053ba`.
- **Source of each finding:** *rendered* means I compared the rendered prototype frame (Playwright, 1440×900) with a rendered Storybook story of the shipped component. Everything else is *code-reading*. Rendered comparisons: A1, B5–B13, C3, C5, C7, C9, C10, C11, D1–D5, D7, D14–D16, E1–E7, E10, F1–F3, F7, F9, F12, F13. ## Patterns behind the drift (fix these and most rows go away)

1. **Defaults no one compared to a frame.** A1 (panel width), C2 (first-row selection), D2 (Board completed = Hidden), E1 (Focus max-width) and E8 (Focus panel view). Each is a single constant, and each changes the whole composition of a frame.
2. **Type one step off, almost always smaller or lighter.** Titles drawn at 600 shipped at 400 (C3, E2, A5). 13–14 px text shipped at 12–13 px (E4, B13, A6). Capture's title is the one that went the other way (F7, 20 px instead of 18 px / 500).
3. **Padding and rows one rung tighter or looser than drawn.** D1 (cards), F6 (subtask rows 36 instead of 28), C15 (Add section 28 instead of 36), B9 (the hairline block 22 instead of 13).
4. **Tone one step louder.** text-2 where the frame used text-3 (B10, B11, B13, C8's lines), and outlined where the frame filled (E3 pill, C11 selected ring, F2 comment cards).
5. **Icons dropped from menus and buttons.** E5, E6, E10, B5, B12, C9.
6. **Section dates exist in the data and the Timeline, but not in the List or Board UI** (C5, C6, D4), despite 68a.
7. **Agent choices that reversed a drawn frame.** They are recorded, but the designer may never have seen them: E8, E9/F22, D6 (W-numbers), B4 (Inbox zero), F9 (the "+" tile).

**A process fix the brief asks about.** Add a "frame check" to `/s2` for UI blocks: render the cited prototype frame and the component's story at 1440×900, side by side, and list each difference as matched, recorded or asked. The scripts this session used could be the start: a prototype frame shooter, a static-Storybook story shooter, and the design-system decision that the `--row-h-sm` chip "matches round-2b frame 7", which shows it can be done.

## Method and evidence

- **Six parallel review lanes**, each reading its frames, the REPLAN calls, the research files they point to, the spec and the later decisions before calling anything drift:
  - A: the shell, the bars and the right panel;
  - B: the sidebar and the Inbox;
  - C: the List;
  - D: Board and Timeline;
  - E: Focus, Upcoming and My tasks;
  - F: details, capture and References.
- **I re-checked the highest-ranked claims in the code myself:**
  - A1: the `20%` defaults;
  - C1: no Target or progress in the centre;
  - C2: `backstopPick` against default p, which turned up the path rule that makes it UNCLEAR;
  - D1: `ItemCard` padding;
  - D2: Completed defaults to hidden;
  - E1: `max-w-2xl`.
- **Rendering:**
  - Every prototype frame and the variants each lane needed were rendered with Playwright at 1440×900.
  - A static Storybook of `develop` was built and its 60+ Tasks and shell stories rendered at the same size.
  - Both sets live in the session's scratchpad; they were not committed.
  - The app itself was not run.
- **Not covered:**
  - light mode and other densities (only the default comfortable density in dark mode);
  - keyboard behaviour beyond reading the key registry;
  - other modules, except where a Tasks frame draws them (F11, Chat previews).
- **Prototype status:** every prototype carries a "Throwaway prototype — not a spec" banner. I counted a drawn detail as approved when a REPLAN call ties to that frame, or when it is the drawn, uncontradicted answer of a decided call. Sample names, numbers and dates were never counted.
- **`.design/tasks-polish/` dates from June 2026,** four months *before* the v3 frames (2026-10-10). Where it disagrees with v3, v3 wins unless a later entry says otherwise. The tag-chip look is the example: June said "coloured #, no dot"; MOD-97/158 (October) chose the dot chip.
