How it would work
For participants: one place, no switching; announcements can't be missed (Catch up + a ping).
For organizers: one workspace to run; Help desk answers are visible to every team.
Costs: 80+ people in a product tuned for 2–5 (pickers, @ menus); teams must move their work out after the event to keep it; per-team privacy relies on private channels, projects and notes being set right by the template.
For participants: their team workspace is theirs and stays after the event (they can keep using Moduo); event news reaches them as a ping and as a count on the workspace switcher.
For organizers: one event workspace for announcements, the help desk and team-finding; they never enter team workspaces unless invited.
Costs: two workspaces per person; Chat needs announcements and Catch up to work across workspaces (a ping and a count, not a merged inbox).
For participants: Moduo is where the team works; Discord is where the event talks.
For organizers: nothing to move; they keep their Discord.
Costs: two tools again (the thing Moduo replaces); no Moduo moment for announcements or the help desk; the smallest MVP.
