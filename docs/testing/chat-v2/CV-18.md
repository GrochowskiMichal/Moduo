## In the app

- [ ] **Do:** Settings → Preferences → Quiet hours on, 19:00 → 9:00; reload; open a second device → **Expect:** it is saved, kept after the reload, and shown on the second device _(web)_
- [ ] **Do:** in your quiet hours, a teammate DMs you → **Expect:** no notification on desktop or in the browser; the message is in Catch up; no email follows later _(desktop + web)_
- [ ] **Do:** a teammate opens your DM during your quiet hours → **Expect:** "Sofia's quiet hours · back 9:00" above the box _(web)_
- [ ] **Do:** a Tasks reminder during quiet hours, then one outside them → **Expect:** in the bell with no push during; a push as before outside _(web)_
- [ ] **Do:** workspace switcher → the bell on a client workspace → **Expect:** it shows paused (bell-off); that workspace's messages don't ring, the other workspace's do; counts still fill _(desktop)_
- [ ] **Do:** start Focus, receive a DM and an @mention → **Expect:** no ring; Catch up shows "2 held until Focus ends"; ending Focus gives one "2 messages while you focused" _(desktop)_
- [ ] **Do:** with a held DM, end Focus with Moduo in the background, then bring Moduo to the front → **Expect:** "1 message while you focused" rings; Catch up opens _(desktop)_
- [ ] **Do:** start Focus, receive a DM, quit the app → **Expect:** within 15 minutes the summary arrives on another device and "held until Focus ends" is gone _(desktop + web)_
- [ ] **Do:** Settings → Chat → Direct messages off → **Expect:** a DM rings nowhere and never emails _(web)_
- [ ] **Do:** change your status many times quickly → **Expect:** after 30 in 10 minutes, "You're changing your status too often" _(web)_

## Edge cases

- [ ] **Do:** set do not disturb, receive a message → **Expect:** the notice is dropped (not held), the message waits in Catch up, and no summary rings later
- [ ] **Do:** pause a workspace you own without a member row → **Expect:** it pauses; leaving a workspace forgets its pause
- [ ] **Do:** pause Focus mid-run → **Expect:** it counts as ending it: what was held is released with one summary
