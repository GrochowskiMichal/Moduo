- **2026-10-08 · Brand system planned: the product is the brand; drawn wordmark; no brand hue; pink isn't a brand colour; the mark reveals itself out of nothing** (designer-decided; Maciej makes every brand call). The brand is the app's own neutral system at a louder volume, with no brand hue, second typeface or illustration style. Geist is the only face; Pilat Extended leaves the font picker. The logo is the mark + the **drawn** lowercase wordmark (the Geist-typed "moduo" in the landing nav, emails and OG image is a stand-in to replace). Maciej is redrawing both masters, so everything downstream is generated from `brand/masters/` and re-exported. Two named blacks: Canvas (pure) and Reading (oklch 0.2, long text). The pink AI dot means "an assistant was here" and has a fixed `--ai` token; whether pink survives anywhere else (accent picker, tag labels, landing demo) is open (❓40, recommended: no). The logo animation follows the mark's own rule: overlaps cancel, so with three strokes stacked the mark is invisible, and the side strokes slide apart to reveal it (once per launch, video intros, landing first visit; never looped). Voice: plain, calm, direct. Brand line "One window for the whole working life."; campaign line "Fire ten apps. Keep the work."; American English; boilerplate names no location (the founders move to Japan before release) and lists chat. Press kit is built now.
