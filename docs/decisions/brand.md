- **2026-10-08 · Pink is not Moduo's accent and there is no "AI disc"; pink stays only as one ordinary accent/tag option** (designer-decided, brand decision 40). Traced because pink "keeps popping up": at the 2026-05-11 foundation grill, a pink circle in Maciej's first-draft screenshot was used to make pink the default accent. The brief written that day relabelled the circle the "AI disc" and made it a rule. It was built as a pink AI button in the bottom bar on 2026-05-13 and removed in the same PR. After that it survived only as copied text (CLAUDE.md until 2026-07-20, PRODUCT_BRIEF §7, landing-agents-readme, the moduo-design-quality skill, the landing's unused `--ai` var); the default accent became mono on 2026-07-27. **Now:** no AI colour, dot or badge anywhere; assistant actions show the assistant's name in the normal neutral style. Pink remains one of the eight Settings accents and tag colours, nothing more. Every guidance copy was removed or annotated in the same change; history docs (REVISION_DELTA, TASKS, DESIGN_REVIEW, audits) were left as history. **Agents: never treat pink as the default, brand or AI colour.** → [.design/brand/DECISIONS.md](../../.design/brand/DECISIONS.md) #40

- **2026-10-08 · Brand system planned: the product is the brand; drawn wordmark; no brand hue; pink isn't a brand colour; the mark reveals itself out of nothing** (designer-decided; Maciej makes every brand call). The brand is the app's own neutral system at a louder volume, with no brand hue, second typeface or illustration style. Geist is the only face; Pilat Extended leaves the font picker. The logo is the mark + the **drawn** lowercase wordmark (the Geist-typed "moduo" in the landing nav, emails and OG image is a stand-in to replace). Maciej is redrawing both masters, so everything downstream is generated from `brand/masters/` and re-exported. Two named blacks: Canvas (pure) and Reading (oklch 0.2, long text). (superseded the same day: there is no AI dot, see the entry above). The logo animation follows the mark's own rule: overlaps cancel, so with three strokes stacked the mark is invisible, and the side strokes slide apart to reveal it (once per launch, video intros, landing first visit; never looped). Voice: plain, calm, direct. Brand line "One window for the whole working life."; campaign line "Fire ten apps. Keep the work."; American English; boilerplate names no location (the founders move to Japan before release) and lists chat. Press kit is built now.
