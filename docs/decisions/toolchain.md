- **2026-10-20 · `optimization.moduleIds: 'compact-hashed'` is not added: Rsbuild 2 already sets it in production.** `@rsbuild/core` applies `chunkIds` and `moduleIds` `'compact-hashed'` to every production build (`isProd && chain.optimization…`). Setting it again through `tools.rspack` left the web bundle byte-identical (JS 7,303,827 B raw, 2,166,557 to 2,166,559 B gzip; first load 1,036.3 KB gzip), and forcing `'named'` to prove the hook works grew the total from 8,770 kB to 8,995 kB. - **2026-10-20 · Rstest stays on the default pool and jsdom: `vmThreads` and `happy-dom` were tried and rejected.** Suite = 490 files, 5,004 tests (same on every run); times are on a loaded Mac (load average 14 to 29), so only the ratios mean anything. Baseline 57 s (56.9, then 58.0 with `pool: threads` named explicitly). `pool: vmThreads` with jsdom: 51 s (about 12% faster) but 3 files fail for realm reasons, not test bugs: `edge/config.test.ts` (4 tests; the vm context has no `crypto.subtle`), `task-description.test.tsx` (4; the lazy Lexical editor never mounts, the textbox is missing) and `new-version-check.test.ts` (1; `Cannot redefine property: location`). `happy-dom` (20.10.6, only present as someone else's dependency) on vmThreads: 51 s, no faster than jsdom, and 6 files fail with 38 tests (`nav-row`, `bucket-rail`, `right-panel`, `task-description`, `use-calendar-module.window`, `analytics.posthog`). The 6 s gain does not pay for rewriting tests around a different realm plus a new undeclared dependency; revisit when the suite passes under `vmThreads` unchanged. → `apps/app/rstest.config.ts`

- **2026-10-20 · Type-checking is back on the official `tsc` (typescript 7.0.2); `tsc-ts` is removed.** The stack audit (docs/local/perf-stack-audit-2026-10.md §5) found `tsc-ts` 0.0.3 is the same tsgo 7.0.2 code with no speed advantage worth an experimental package: `tsc --noEmit` on the whole `typecheck` graph (8 workspaces, forced, no cache) took 3.9 s against 2.4 s on `tsc-ts`, and the app alone 2.3 s. - **2026-10-20 · Shortcuts are one typed registry on TanStack Hotkeys (TS-6); a page beats a global key by scope, not by listener order.** `@tanstack/react-hotkeys` 0.16.0 (core `@tanstack/hotkeys` 0.17.0) and `@tanstack/react-hotkeys-devtools` 0.11.0 are pinned exactly. `apps/app/src/lib/hotkeys/registry.ts` lists every document-level shortcut with its scope: `global`, a route (`tasks`, `chat`, `notes`, `calendar`, `contacts`, `home`, `email`, turned on in `route-tree.tsx` by `withHotkeyScope`), a surface inside a route (`task-page`, `focus`, `catchup`), `panel` (the right panel) and `modal` (the capture shell). Components bind with `useAppHotkeys({ id: handler })`; every binding listens on `document` in the bubble phase, so React's own `onKeyDown` handlers and Radix's document-capture Esc run first, and `runHotkey` skips a key they claimed (`defaultPrevented`) and any key typed into a field or a Lexical editor unless the binding says `inInputs` (⌘K, ⌘⇧K and the capture's ⌘1–7). The library's own `ignoreInputs`, `preventDefault` and `stopPropagation` are off: its `ignoreInputs` also swallows arrows inside ARIA grids and listboxes, which would change Tasks and Calendar. When two active scopes bind one key the library fires only the narrowest (nested, else most recently activated), so a handler that returns `false` does **not** fall through to the wider binding; a binding that only applies sometimes is gated with `enabled` (the right panel's Esc and the Focus view are on only while focus is inside them, via `useFocusWithin`). `registry.test.ts` fails on two bindings for one key in one scope (both platforms, `Mod` resolved) and on a narrower scope reusing a wider scope's key without `overrides: true` (email's `?`, the capture's ⌘1–7 are the two today). The capture-phase override pattern is gone. Left element-local on purpose: focused-widget `onKeyDown` handlers (the Tasks list's j/k, menus, pickers), Lexical commands, gesture-time listeners (drags, the timeline canvas, the task-line plugin) and Mindmap's window listeners (it has no route scope yet). The Hotkeys panel sits in the dev-only TanStack Devtools. → `apps/app/src/lib/hotkeys/` · `apps/app/src/lib/shortcuts.ts` · `apps/app/src/lib/devtools/tanstack-devtools.tsx` · [specs/tanstack-spine.md](../../specs/tanstack-spine.md)
