## Scope

Chat v2 makes the Chat module **complete and pleasant for hackathon teams and for teams of 2–5 on the Duo and Team plans: founders, developers, PMs and agencies working with clients**, complete meaning each can run a normal week (or a hackathon weekend) without Slack, Discord, Teams chat or a WhatsApp group for work (REPLAN §3; [`research/role-journeys.md`](../.design/chat-v2/research/role-journeys.md)). It was re-planned because v1 was "a Slack built beside Moduo": every reversal the audit found had one shape, v1 rebuilding Slack's way a piece Moduo now has once for the whole app (REPLAN §1.1).

It delivers, in one spec:
- **Chat reaches you.** What's addressed to you (a direct message, `@you`, a reply in a chain you're in, an announcement) lands as an OS or browser notification when Moduo isn't in front of you, in any workspace, from one pipeline shared with Tasks; one device rings; an email after an hour away; Focus, quiet hours, do not disturb and a paused workspace hold everything, and it all waits in **Catch up**, one list you can finish (CH6, CH7, CH8, CH38–CH46, CH73, CH74).
- **Chat knows the work.** A channel links to a project, a client or a team and shows on it; anything you mention is a live reference; a message becomes a task through the app's capture with the link both ways; decisions are marked and show on the items they're about; `MOD-142` typed in a message is a live chip (CH1, CH2, CH10, CH11, CH13, CH52, CH66).
- **Replies, not threads.** One Reply action, replies in the stream with a quote, "N replies ›" to read a chain on the right, Wrap up by hand with the reply that settled it, tidy-up afterwards (Reply to…, move, make a task or a note), a Reply chains view (CH30, CH30c, CH30d, CH53–CH55).
- **The new look** on Tasks' kit: Slack-calm as the base, 15 px reading text, hierarchy by weight, the two-speed rhythm, centred date separators, the "New" pill, a one-row composer that grows, green = reachable and grey shapes for the rest, one glyph per channel (CH27–CH29g, CH32).
- **The platform underneath**, kept and extended: v1's tables and ops; a chat store on the shared store pattern with a device copy, delta sync, zero requests per incoming message and an outbox; messages stored as a Markdown subset with `moduo://` references and drawn by a pure parser; TanStack Virtual; full-text search that finds Polish; typing scoped to a conversation's members; Chat in ⌘K, the panel registry, the capture registry, the notification table, the lossless export and the MCP connector (REPLAN §6).
- **The hackathon cut**, drawn as a line in the blocks: what a team of four needs for a weekend in a handed-out Team workspace (CH22, CH25, CH26a, CH67, CH68).

**Why now:** a hackathon Moduo may help organize gives Chat its first concrete use; each team gets a prepared workspace on a dated Team plan, and chat is what makes it their home for 48 hours. v1 is live and its screens are a placeholder nothing visual carries over from (Maciej, 2026-10-13).

**Landing rule:** LOCAL BUILD MODE (above) until "release"; then the release train applies the migrations in order (`email-worker` with the `chat_waiting` template deployed **before** the migration that queues it; `unaccent` checked on prod before the foundation applies; the conversion applied once), the MCP connector redeploys, `min_build` is raised, and Supabase Pro is decided from spike 3's numbers (CH31).

**Not restated here:** the module's job-to-be-done and the boundary with Notes (REPLAN §1.5: a discussion about an item lives on the item; chat owns conversations; both link through References), the interaction kit (DS-6), the attachments pipeline (`specs/attachments.md`), the shared store pattern (`specs/tasks-v3.md` §Assumptions #7), the notification channels (TV-D12) and Time & region (TV-D14).

## Product behavior & UX

Element-level detail (sizes, exact words, per-state frames) lives in round 5 of the prototypes and the research files; each subsection names its frame. What follows is the behaviour two people would build the same way.

### 1. Words, shape and who sees Chat (CH1–CH5, CH3a, CH4a, CH32, CH37)

- **Words:** the module is **Chat**; any channel or direct message is a **conversation**; a named one is a **channel**; one between two to nine people is a **direct message**. No `#` in front of channel names (`#` is tags). "Pin to sidebar" for conversations; "Pin to conversation" for messages. Notes-to-self conversations don't exist: picking only yourself in New conversation offers "Capture a note".
- **Channel names are as typed** ("Acme rebrand", "Launch v1"): spaces and capitals allowed, 1–80 characters, trimmed, unique in the workspace ignoring case among live channels (CH3a). A channel's icon is **one glyph for every channel, plus a lock for a private one you're in**; what it's linked to shows in its header chip and Details, never as an icon (CH32).
- **The Chat tab shows when a workspace has two or more members** (CH4); a one-person workspace never shows it, and a workspace back to one member keeps it while chat history exists. Chat is **on** when the owner's plan includes it (Duo, Team, Founder) or the workspace holds a dated grant (CH25, CH67); otherwise the tab opens on the paused state below (with the history, when there is any). ⌘7 stays Chat's number.
- **When the plan doesn't include chat** (a lapsed Duo, a grant that ended): Chat stays in the top bar, **every conversation stays readable and searchable**, and the composer is replaced by one line: "Chat is paused: this workspace's plan doesn't include it. Anna can turn it back on." The owner sees the same line with "Choose a plan". No marketing page (CH4a). Reading, marking read and unread, clearing Catch up, Later and Remind me, reacting, notification levels, Pin to sidebar, leaving, presence and statuses keep working, as do search, the Home widgets and read-only MCP; posting, editing, deleting, creating channels, files, moving, linking, decisions, pins and custom emoji wait for the plan.
- **The 3×3 layout** (Tasks 48b): left = conversations, centre = the open conversation, right = context; the bottom bar holds Chat's tools (Search · Capture · New conversation) and each panel's toggle; Catch up and a multi-select swap the bottom bar's centre for a mode row (Tasks 82b). No rails, strips or extra columns. Frame 1 of round 5 shows the whole.
- **A new workspace's first open** (CH37): "General" exists, everyone is in it, and the conversation shows a one-line welcome ("Reply to keep answers together · @ links anything in Moduo") that disappears after the first message. Frame 7.

### 2. The left panel (CH5, CH8, CH28b) — frame 1

Order: **Catch up** (with its count) · hairline · **Pinned** (only when something is pinned) · **Channels** (alphabetical) · **Direct messages** (most recent first) · "+ New conversation". GroupHeaders are the kit's, sentence case, collapsible and remembered. **Unread is weight** (the name turns bold); **a number appears only for what's addressed to you** (a direct message's unread, your mentions and replies in a channel, every message in a channel you set to "Every message"); one count style, never a coloured pill; a muted conversation ("Nothing") still turns bold and never counts. Presence shows on direct-message rows and the person card (§10). Archived channels and Recently deleted open from the panel's ⋯, never as permanent rows.

### 3. The conversation: toolbar, stream and message row (CH27–CH29f, CH29d, §6 #17, #18) — frames 5, 6, 7

- **Toolbar** (the kit's grammar): the glyph · the name · the linked item's chip with its live state (project colour · name · status; a client's company; a team's mark) · the people count · Search · ⋯. An announcement channel says so under the name.
- **The stream** is one virtualized list (TanStack Virtual; `MessageStream`) that opens at the bottom, follows new messages when you're there, never pulls while you read history, and loads older pages by `(created_at, id)` so nothing goes missing at a page boundary. **Day separators are centred** on a hairline, in the shared date grammar extended with the recent past: Today · Yesterday · Mon (up to six days back) · Oct 9 · Oct 9, 2025 (CH29c). The top and bottom edges fade the text (CH29). A deep link or a search result loads a window around the message and shows a jump bar ("Viewing messages from Oct 2 · Jump to present").
- **"New" since you last looked:** a small **"New" pill centred on a hairline** at the read mark, no row tint; when you're scrolled up and a message arrives, a **floating "N new ↓" chip** appears and scrolls you down (CH29d). The read mark is **the last message that was on screen**, never "now", and it moves forward only (§6 #17).
- **The message row** (Slack-calm base, CH29): a 32 px round two-initial avatar with a stable colour (28 / 24 at compact / dense), the name at the body size but heavier, the time small and grey; **body text 15 px at every density** (CH29a); **24 px before a new person (20 / 16 by density), 6 px between one person's lines** (CH29f); one left edge for text, reactions, files and reply quotes; flat rows, hover = a see-through fill; the hover time sits on the first line's centre; media gets the same space below as above. Mentions of others are a light grey mark; `@you` is the one solid accent mark in text; links are underlined, monochrome. Emoji-only messages draw large ("jumbo"). An edited message shows "(edited)"; a deleted one without replies disappears, one with replies reads "Deleted message".
- **The hover toolbar** floats over the row's top edge: your first three quick reactions · any emoji · Reply · Task · ⋯ (CH29b; Reply before Task, Maciej's deferral). ⋯ holds: Edit (own) · Delete (own; others' with the switch) · Pin to conversation · Mark as decision · Reply to… · Move to… · Make a task · Make a note · Remind me · Copy link · Mark unread. Right-click opens the same menu with all six quick reactions on top.
- **Edit and delete:** ↑ in an empty composer edits your last message; an edit that names someone new notifies only them (CH75). Delete hides the message at once with **"Deleted · Undo" for 8 s**, no confirm (§6 #10); after the toast the text, mentions, reactions and pin are blanked on the server and files go to the trash clock.
- **Sending states** (frame 6): your message is visible under 50 ms after ⏎; offline it sits in place as **"Waiting to send"** and sends itself in order when you're back (CH64); only when the server refuses it does it show **"Not sent · Retry"** (a viewer, an announcement channel, a paused plan). Reactions, edits, deletes and pins while offline say "Offline".
- **Density** (CH27): one layout; the app's density setting tightens avatars, gutters and spacing; no chat-only compact toggle.

### 4. The composer and the grammar (CH14, CH15, CH29g, CH66, §6 #3) — frames 5, 6

- **One row that grows:** at rest `+` · the text · `@` · emoji · send; past one line the text takes the full width and the tools drop into a row below. Formatting buttons appear only when text is selected (⌘B ⌘I ⌘E as today); Markdown shortcuts work as you type (`**`, `` ` ``, `>`); pasting stays clean text; ⏎ sends (never during IME composition), ⇧⏎ makes a new line. Drafts are kept per conversation and chain on the device.
- **`+` is the one way in for everything that isn't text:** upload a file; link something (tabs All · Tasks · Notes · Events · People, recent first); create a linked task, note or event. Every way to connect lands as the same chip: `@`, `/task` `/note` `/event`, `+`, pasting a Moduo link, dragging an item from the panel or another module onto the box.
- **The grammar** (Tasks 33, RF-1): `@` finds people first, then things (teams, projects, tasks, notes, contacts, events, emails, other conversations); `#` is tags; `/` runs commands (`/task`, `/note`, `/event`, `/today`, `/tomorrow`, `/date`, `/remind`); `:` opens emoji with the workspace's custom ones first. **A typed task handle (`MOD-142`) becomes a live chip as you type**, like a date chip; Esc or ⌘Z keeps the text (CH66). `@channel` reaches everyone in the channel once, only for roles that may "Mention everyone"; `@Team` notifies the team once each (CH7).
- **What's stored** is a Markdown subset with `moduo://` references, never a title; rows are drawn by a pure parser whose tree is cached per message; the composer is the shared Lexical editor with RF-1's menus (§6 #3).

### 5. Replies, chains, Wrap up and tidying up (CH30a, CH30c, CH30d, CH53–CH55) — frame 5

- **One action, Reply.** A reply posts at the bottom, in time order, visible to everyone, with one muted line quoting what it answers (click it to jump). No "Reply in thread", no second reply button. A reply to a reply joins the same chain.
- **A message with replies shows "4 replies · Alex, Mike ›"**; clicking opens the whole chain in the right panel as an item with a back arrow (root, replies in order, its own reply box, "Stop following").
- **Wrap up, by hand:** a "Wrap up" button in the chain's header and in the root's ⋯. It asks **"Which reply settled it?"** and offers **"Also mark as decision"** (ticked by default in a linked channel, CH53). The replies **leave the main stream** (a drawing rule: they stay rows for unread, search and export) and the root shows "✓ Wrapped up by Sofia: Jana 'Got them…' · 4 replies ›". Undo for 8 s; "Reopen" in the chain. **A new reply reopens it visibly** ("Reopened by Mike", CH54). Who may wrap up: the root's author, the channel's managers and admins; in a direct message, anyone in it who can post.
- **Reply chains** is a right-panel view: every open chain in this conversation, newest activity first, with "1 new"; wrapped ones below.
- **Tidy up afterwards:** **Reply to…** attaches a stray message to a chain; **Move to…** moves selected messages to another conversation (your own anywhere; others' only by managers and admins, and only to a same-or-narrower audience); when people in the target couldn't read the source, a warning first ("2 people who aren't in Founders will see this", CH55). A quiet event line in both conversations says who moved what; Undo for 8 s; nothing pings again. **Make a task** (§8); **Make a note** (Notes accepted it, N19: a note in your Notes Inbox quoting the messages with authors and times, titled with the conversation's name and date); **Move to a task's comments** when Tasks accepts the proposal.
- **No automatic folding** in v2 (it can come back as a setting).

### 6. Reactions and emoji (CH16, CH16a, CH16b, CH29b, CH49, CH60) — frame 5

- **Your own quick row** (six by default: 👍 ✅ 👀 🙏 ❤️ 😄), set in Settings → Chat or from "Customize…" at the end of the hover row; the hover shows the first three; any emoji behind ☺; the full picker (Frimousse, native emoji) with the workspace's custom emoji first. **The same row and picker apply to task and note comments** (CH16a, CH49). Reactions never notify. Viewers react (CH60). Up to 20 kinds per message.
- **Custom workspace emoji** (CH16b, right after the hackathon cut): any member unless a role switch restricts it; PNG, GIF or WebP up to 256 KB, shown up to 128 px; `:name:`; animated ones play only on hover and never with reduced motion; Settings → Chat → Custom emoji lists them with who added each, admins remove any; part of the export.

### 7. Files (CH17) — frames 5, 9

Paste, drop or attach through the app's attachments pipeline (AT-2): images and PDFs preview inline (a size cap keeps the stream calm), everything else is a file chip; up to 10 files per message; limits follow the owner's plan or the grant's. A file's access follows its message's conversation; deleting the message sends its files to the trash clock. Files are listed in the conversation's **Files** view. Before uploads land in prod, the stop-gap is files as task attachments or links (CH68).

### 8. Chat knows the work (CH2, CH10, CH11, CH13, CH52, CH56, CH57, §6 #4, #5) — frames 4, 5

- **A channel links to at most one thing: a project, a client (company or contact) or a team.** "Link to a project, client or team" in Details; **"Start a conversation for X"** in a project's ⋯ and on a client's hub creates a channel named after X, private with X's people when X is private, public otherwise (CH56). Linking a team offers once "Add the team's 4 people?", never kept in sync (CH57). The toolbar shows the item's live chip; Details shows its state; capture from the channel files into the linked project.
- **The item shows its conversation:** "Conversation: Acme · 3 new" on the project overview and the client's hub (TV-U16's slot), and a quiet **"Mentioned in Acme · Oct 12"** line in any item's Linked when a message mentions it (one line per conversation; only readers of that conversation see it). No "Discuss in chat" button on items.
- **A message becomes a task:** `T` on a message or ⋯ → Make a task opens the app's capture as Task with the message's words as the title draft, a removable **"From: Sofia's message in Acme"** chip and the destination set to the linked project (Inbox otherwise); ⏎ creates it and the message shows **"Became a task: ○ Get logo SVGs · Today"**, live; the task's Linked shows the message. `/task` does the same from a line you're typing. Files stay on the message.
- **Decisions:** "Mark as decision" (⋯ or `⇧D`) gives a message a quiet "Decision" label, lists it in Details' Decisions, and shows it on the items it's linked to or mentions ("Decided with Mike · Oct 12: …"), to the people who can read that conversation, even from a direct message (CH52). Not a log, not a workflow.
- **References:** two new kinds. A **message** (chip: author · first words; card: author · time · conversation · two lines; "Deleted message") and a **conversation** (chip: glyph · name; card: name · linked item · people · last message). "Private item" for anything you can't read, never with a title. In running text a referenced item is a chip plus a collapsible preview card (RF-1's chat default).
- **The Message capture (⌘7 inside the capture modal):** no default destination: the picker opens focused, recent conversations and people first; a person starts a direct message; a "From:" item becomes a reference card in the message; ⏎ sends and shows "Sent to Acme · Open · Undo" (Undo deletes it within 8 s) (CH65).

### 9. Catch up (CH8, CH38–CH41, CH51, CH61, CH73) — frame 1

- **One list of what's addressed to you**, grouped by conversation (newest first is a Display option): direct messages, `@you` and `@Team`, replies in chains you're in and to your own messages, announcements, a conversation set to "Every message" as one line ("Acme · 12 new", CH73), Remind me items when due, and quiet lines (added to or removed from a channel, the conversion's level reset). It's a mode: the bottom bar shows **Reply `R` · Done `Space` · Task `T` · Later `H` · Open `⏎` · More**, then "4 left · Esc"; the selected item opens on the right as the message in context (ten lines either side, "Open in conversation"). Keys on a grouped item act on its newest message.
- **Done** clears it; reading it in its conversation clears it too; **the bell and Catch up are one state** (a mention cleared in either is gone from both, CH38). **Later** brings it back at a time you pick (in 1 hour · this evening · tomorrow · pick); **Remind me** on any message does the same from its ⋯ (CH51); `/remind` in the composer reminds you about the conversation. The ⋯ offers "Done all in this conversation" with Undo.
- **Empty:** "You're caught up. Nothing is waiting for you. Three channels have messages you weren't asked about; they'll keep."
- **The rules behind the counts:** an `@you` in a conversation set to "Nothing" waits here silently, no ping, no count (CH40); when quiet hours, do not disturb, a vacation or Focus end, nothing pings and everything waits here (CH41).

### 10. Presence, statuses and Focus (CH9, CH28a–CH28d, CH42, CH45, CH47, CH48, CH70) — frames 4, 8

- **Green means one thing: reachable now** (Moduo open, not idle, not on do not disturb). Everything else is a grey shape: an **outline** for away (after 10 minutes idle; no "since" time, CH48), a **thick bar** for do not disturb (by hand, sick, vacation), a **target mark** for Focusing, **nothing** for offline. Presence shows where you'd message someone (direct-message rows, the person card on hover anywhere, the people in Details) and on Home, never in the top bar. "Show me as online" is a personal switch, on by default.
- **Focusing:** teammates see "Focusing" (plus "until 3:00 PM" only during a Pomodoro phase or a counted-down estimate, CH42); the task's title only if you turn on "Show what I'm focusing on" and they can see that task (CH47). Focus holds chat's pings until the session ends, then they arrive together: "3 messages while you focused".
- **Statuses** (CH28d, CH45): an emoji + short text + "clear after" (30 min · 1 h · today · this week · a date · never) + optionally "do not disturb while it's on"; Moduo's presets In a meeting · Out sick · On vacation · Commuting, and your own saved ones ("Sleeping until 6"); **one status for all your workspaces**; set from your avatar in the top bar. It shows in the direct message's header, on a line above the box ("Jana is on vacation until Oct 20. She won't be notified; your message waits in her Catch up"), the person card and Details' people; Chat adds it to the shared person picker, so Tasks' assign picker shows "Jana · on vacation until Oct 20" (BUILD_ORDER 🟠 box). During your quiet hours, teammates who message you see "Sofia's quiet hours · back 9:00" on the same line above the box (CH7).
- **Home:** Chat supplies each person's "now" line (Active · Focusing · Away · the status) to the one **Team** widget, above Tasks' load rows (CH70); **Catch up** is Chat's own widget (CH35).

### 11. Notifications: the table, all of it (CH6, CH7, CH33, CH34, CH39–CH46, CH73–CH75, §6 #6, #23) — frame 9

Chat adds rows to the app's one pipeline (TV-D12) and never builds its own sender. A device notice reaches you **only when Moduo isn't in front of you** (another app, another module or another workspace counts), **in any workspace**, with the workspace's name; a message's push goes out **at once** from the write, not on the minute; **only one device rings** (your most recently active client; a client that has your attention shows the message in place and acknowledges); a browser rings only when nothing acknowledged within 8 s; a burst in one conversation replaces the last notice ("Sofia · 3 messages"); a click lands on the message. What a notice shows: the sender and the conversation, and the text, with a switch "Show message text" for people who share their screen (CH33); references are drawn as names you can see. **Sound:** one soft sound for direct messages and `@you`, off by default (CH34). **The web app asks once**, the first time you open Chat in a browser ("Get a notice when someone messages you · Allow · Not now"); refused, Catch up says notifications are off in this browser (CH44). **"Count":** a conversation's number is how many of its unread messages are addressed to you (on "Every message", every unread one); the Chat tab, the workspace switcher and the dock or taskbar badge count open Catch up items (an "Every message" conversation is one item), per workspace and summed. **The switches** in Settings → Notifications are Direct messages · Mentions and replies · Announcements · Every message · each key's notifications; a switch turns off the device notice and the email for its rows and nothing else (the bell row and the Catch up item are always written).

## Edge cases

Each becomes a test (next sections). Grouped by what fails.

**Sending, reading, the stream**
- ⏎ while offline → the message sits in place as "Waiting to send" with its real time once sent; reconnecting sends the outbox in order with the same client-minted ids (no duplicate on a retry); a refusal (viewer, announcement channel, paused plan) turns it into "Not sent · Retry" and never into a silently vanished message.
- 200 unread when a channel opens → only the messages that were on screen become read; the read mark moves to the last one seen; the "New" pill sits before the first unseen one and never jumps as older pages load.
- Two messages share a timestamp at a page boundary → both appear exactly once (pages key on time and id).
- A 60 s disconnect with more than 50 new messages → after reconnecting every gap is filled by a delta read before the window shows; nothing is missing for good.
- A message arrives while you're scrolled up → the floating "N new ↓" chip; the anchor row doesn't move; clicking scrolls down and clears the chip.
- A deep link or a search hit to a message from last month → the stream loads a window around it, highlights it, shows the jump bar; the chain opens beside it when the message is a reply.
- ⏎ during IME composition (Polish, Japanese input) → nothing sends; ⏎ after composition sends.
- An edit adds `@Anna` → Anna hears once; nobody else hears about the edit; an edit that removes a mention sends nothing.
- Delete then Undo within 8 s → the message is back with its reactions and files; after 8 s the server blanks the text, mentions, reactions and pin and the files start the trash clock; a deleted root with replies reads "Deleted message" and keeps its chain.
- A message of 8,000 stored characters (references included) → refused with the count, nothing partial; a message of 10 files → the eleventh is refused before upload.
- A 10,000-message conversation → opens warm in ≤ 100 ms with the newest 50 from the device copy, draws only what's on screen (≤ ~3,000 elements), scrolls without a frame over 50 ms; switching back is instant (measurement cache).
- Density switches while a conversation is open → the anchor row moves ≤ 1 px.

**Replies, chains, moving**
- A reply to a reply → joins the root's chain, quotes the reply it answers; "N replies" counts it.
- Wrap up on a chain with unread replies → the replies leave the stream; the unread ones stay in Catch up as one chain item; search and export still find them.
- A new reply to a wrapped chain → "Reopened by Mike" on the root; the replies come back in place.
- Wrap up by someone who isn't the root's author, a manager or an admin → the button isn't offered; the op refuses.
- Moving your own messages into a conversation whose readers couldn't read the source → the op answers "2 people who aren't in Founders will see this" first; confirmed, the messages move, with an event line in both conversations and one Undo.
- Moving someone else's messages as a manager into a wider audience → refused; into the same or a narrower audience → allowed.
- Moving a root → its whole chain moves; moving a reply alone → it leaves its chain; pins are dropped; files follow.
- "Reply to…" on a message already in a chain → re-parents it; the old chain's count drops.

**Catch up and notifications**
- A mention in a conversation set to "Nothing" → in Catch up, no notice, no count, not in the bell's count.
- A mention cleared in the bell → gone from Catch up; Done in Catch up → dismissed in the bell; read in the conversation → both cleared.
- A direct message while the desktop app is in front → no notice; shown in place; the browser tab doesn't ring either (acknowledged).
- A direct message with the desktop app open on another module and a browser tab in the background → exactly one notice (the most recently active client); none from the browser.
- Three messages from Sofia in a minute → one notice that updates ("Sofia · 3 messages"), one Catch up group, a count of 3.
- A notice from another workspace → it says the workspace's name; clicking it switches workspace and lands on the message; a running Focus keeps running.
- Quiet hours, do not disturb or a vacation end → nothing pings; what arrived waits in Catch up; no email goes out for it.
- A Focus session ends with held pings → one summary notice; the items are in Catch up.
- An unread direct message, an hour old, Moduo closed for an hour → one email; a second message in the same conversation within the hour → no second email; "Email when away" off → none; a paused workspace → none.
- Browser permission refused → Catch up says notifications are off in this browser; the setting reads "Not allowed by the browser"; the bell still fills.
- A conversation set to "Every message" → one notice per burst only when Moduo isn't in front; one Catch up line per conversation; the sidebar counts every message.
- An `@channel` from a role without "Mention everyone" → sent as plain text, nobody is pinged, the composer says so.
- A reply to a post your agent made → reaches you only through that key's notification switch, unless the reply mentions you.
- A reminder set on a message for next week → it returns to the top of Catch up at that time with one notice; "Remind me" beyond a year → refused.
- 300 items after a week away → Catch up stays grouped and fast; "Done all in this conversation" with Undo; Done on a group clears its messages' bell twins.

**Channels, permissions, people**
- A viewer → reads, reacts, receives direct messages, can't post or start one; the composer shows a line, not a box; Catch up's `R` opens nothing to type into.
- A member posts in an announcement channel → refused with "Only managers post here · Reply privately"; a manager's post pings everyone once.
- A public channel made private → non-members lose it (their Catch up items for it close); keys lose it unless "Include private channels I'm in" and the creator is a member; it never goes back to public.
- Someone is removed from a private channel → a quiet Catch up line; the channel leaves their sidebar; they can't rejoin unless added; the channel's messages stop arriving in their store within one access check.
- A channel is deleted → Recently deleted for 30 days (managers and admins see it; Restore brings everything back); the purge is final; General refuses.
- The last manager leaves → the channel passes to the workspace owner if they can read it, else its earliest remaining member; a manager can't un-manage themselves while last.
- Linking a team → "Add the team's 4 people?" once; later team changes don't follow.
- "Start a conversation for X" when X is private → a private channel with exactly the people who can see X; when X is public → a public channel; a name clash → "Acme 2".
- The linked project is archived or deleted → the channel stays; the header chip becomes RF-1's tombstone; capture from it falls back to the Inbox.
- A former member → their messages keep their name with a quiet label; their direct message reads "Jana left the workspace"; a deleted account reads "Former member".
- Channel names: "Launch v1" and "launch v1" → the second is refused as a duplicate; 81 characters → refused; a name of spaces → refused.

**Several workspaces, plans, grants**
- A person in two workspaces with chat → statuses, quiet hours and do not disturb apply to both; the switcher counts each; the dock badge is the sum.
- A workspace's plan lapses → every conversation stays readable and searchable; the composer shows the paused line; the owner sees "Choose a plan"; Catch up, widgets and read-only MCP keep working; a queued "Waiting to send" message becomes "Not sent · Retry".
- A dated grant ends during a hackathon → the same paused state; no new invites (the hackathon plan's rule); everything stays readable.
- The grant mechanism (TX-9b) isn't built yet → chat's plan check falls back to the owner's plan and works unchanged.

**Converting live v1 data**
- A v1 message with `<@id>`, `<!channel>`, `<moduo:TYPE:ID|Label>` and `_italic_` → one stored v2 body with the person URI, the `@channel` token, `moduo://TYPE/ID` and `*italic*`; drawn identically by the client parser; running the conversion again changes zero rows.
- A v1 thread → a chain whose replies sit in the stream at their own times, counted as read for whoever had read the root.
- A notes-to-self conversation → one private note "Notes to self (from Chat)" in the person's Notes Inbox with every message in order; the conversation is gone from the sidebar.
- A channel on "Every message" set by v1 for its creator → reset to Mentions and replies with one quiet Catch up line; direct messages keep notifying.
- A v1 slug name (`acme-launch`) → kept until someone renames it.
- A desktop build from before the release → its chat sends still work through the v1-signature wrappers for one release; after `min_build` is raised it's told to update and runs read-only.

**Agents, search, export**
- A key with "Include private channels I'm in" on → reads those channels only while its creator is a member; the channel's Details say an app can read it; a direct message never appears to any key.
- An agent posting 31 times in a minute → the 31st is refused; an agent using `@channel` for a creator who may not → sent as plain text.
- Searching "zolw" → finds "żółw"; "zadan" → "zadania"; a private channel's message never appears for a non-member or an outsider; 100,000 messages → p95 ≤ 150 ms.
- The export → every conversation you can read, chains, reactions, custom emoji and attachments **as files**, JSON plus a Markdown per conversation; a deleted account's rows erase every new per-person table.

## Assumptions & technical decisions

Decided by the agent (REPLAN §6, round 3); each line is decision · why · rejected. The durable ones are entries in `docs/decisions/chat.md`. Mike's review copy with every column, op and index is [`round-3-database.md`](../.design/chat-v2/research/round-3-database.md).

1. **v1's data shape stays** (§6 #1): `chat_channels`, `chat_members`, `chat_messages`, `chat_channel_managers`, `workspace_chat_caps`; every write a `chat_op_*`; reactions and chain rollups on the message row; soft deletes; nothing renamed (`parent_id` now means the chain's root, `starred` is "Pin to sidebar"). *Why:* sound, and a rename breaks every function body and the deployed connector for nothing a person sees. *Rejected:* rebuilding the tables; child tables for reactions (a Realtime delete carries no row-level security).
2. **A `ChatStore` on the shared store pattern** (§6 #2, TV-D11a): one IndexedDB row per message (the newest ~500 per conversation on the device, ≤ ~1,500 in memory), keyset pages by `(created_at, id)`, delta reads by a server-stamped `updated_at` (new on all three tables, by trigger), Realtime applied from the event with zero requests, echo skip by id, one-write rollback, an outbox for sends and uploads drained in order, an access check (`chat_sync_ids`) on rejoin, focus, every 10 minutes and on a membership change. Typing and presence in their own small stores. *Rejected:* v1's per-page fetch and resync; a generic sync framework; SQLite WASM.
3. **Messages are stored as a Markdown subset with `moduo://` references** (§6 #3; `format_version = 2`), never a title; `body_text` (syntax and URIs stripped) and `search_tsv` are kept by trigger; rows are drawn by a pure parser cached per message id + `updated_at`; the composer is the shared Lexical editor with RF-1's menus and a restricted shortcut list; the limit is 8,000 stored characters, references included. *Rejected:* Lexical JSON, an editor per row, server HTML, a v1 parser kept forever, a second "visible length" limit.
4. **Client-minted message ids are the idempotency key**; `client_id` stays for v1 rows. The v1 signatures of `chat_op_send` and `chat_op_edit` stay **one release** as wrappers that convert v1 markup and call the v2 op; `min_build` is raised at the release and the wrappers are dropped after (CV-23). *Rejected:* dropping them at once (every not-yet-updated desktop's send fails).
5. **References** (§6 #4): kinds *conversation* (`moduo://chat_channel/<id>`) and *message* (`moduo://chat_message/<id>`); every conversation registers in `entities` on create (a channel's name as the label; none for a direct message); a message registers **only when something links to it**, without a label; ⌘K's Messages come from `chat_search`, not from registry rows. *Rejected:* registry rows for every message (a label would carry message text).
6. **Spine links** (§6 #5): `chat_channels.linked_type/linked_id` (one per channel; closed: `bucket`, `company`, `contact`, `team`) mirrored by an `entity_links` row of a new relation `conversation-of` (a team has no registry row, so a team link is the column only); send, edit and move write one `mentions` link per conversation and referenced item, kept as history. Every registry, activity and link row about a conversation or a message is readable only by people who can read that conversation (`perm_can_see_entity`, `spine__visible_to` and the `entity_links` read rule gain chat branches, in the foundation, before any such row is written). *Rejected:* a link per message; `entity_links` alone for the channel link.
7. **Notifications through TV-D12's pipeline** (§6 #6; round-3 §2.14): the fan-out runs inside `chat_op_send` / `_edit` / `_agent_post` and writes, per recipient who can read the conversation, a `chat_catchup` row, a bell row where the table says so and a `notice_deliveries` row through one gate (`notice__gate` → send · hold · drop); a kick trigger pushes chat kinds at once and `push-send` drains a burst, the minute job sweeps; `user_clients` (stamped from the session) decides which device rings and whether to email; `notice_op_ack` acknowledges; web push rings only after 8 s unacknowledged; bursts share a tag per conversation; the email is a 5-minute job queueing `chat_waiting` through TX-3's outbox (template deployed first); an item written while the gate answered *drop* (quiet hours, do not disturb, a vacation, a paused workspace) is stamped `held_at` and the job never emails it (CH74). Device notices' kinds: `chat_message`, `chat_mention`, `chat_reply`, `chat_announcement`, `chat_reminder`, `chat_focus_summary`; bell ops: `chat.mention` (reason person · team · everyone), `chat.reply`, `chat.announcement`. *Rejected:* a chat-only notifier; polling; a 5-second cron; queueing the email at send and cancelling on read.
8. **Catch up is its own per-person table** (`chat_catchup`: reason, done_how, later_until, emailed_at, held_at, the bell twin's activity id), published and filtered by person; the bell and Catch up are one state through the twin; Later and Remind me are one mechanism (`chat_op_remind`, a minute job, a one-year horizon). *Rejected:* deriving Catch up from the bell (direct messages aren't bell rows; Later needs a per-person row).
9. **Search** (§6 #7): `unaccent` + an immutable wrapper, `to_tsvector('simple', …)` as a stored generated column with a GIN index, `chat_search` (prefix matching, readers only via `chat_can_read_channel`, newest first, `ts_headline`, ≤ 50 rows) used by ⌘K, the conversation's Search and MCP. *Rejected:* substring match, `pg_trgm` now, PGroonga, an external engine, on-device search.
10. **Panel and capture registries** (§6 #8): `PanelModule` gains `chat` with views Details · Reply chains · Files (*about this*, ⌥1–3) and items (a chain, a person, a message in context, any reference); `CaptureModule` `chat` registers the Message type (⌘7). *Rejected:* chat-only panel modes.
11. **General on the server** (§6 #9): `is_default` on one channel per workspace; a trigger on `workspace_members` joins new members (unless they left before: leaving is a `left_at` stamp, never a deleted row); `chat_op_bootstrap` covers older workspaces. *Rejected:* the per-device flag.
12. **Delete with Undo** (§6 #10): hide at once; a minute job blanks text, mentions, reactions and pin once 8 s passed; files to the trash clock; `chat_op_restore` inside the window. *Rejected:* the confirm toast.
13. **PERM-7 made honest** (§6 #11, #27): ops read `chat_channel_managers` and "Manage any channel"; new ops for managers, the announcement switch, make private, delete and restore; the chat switches live once in the role editor plus `add_emoji`; "Start calls" stays a column and leaves the screens. The permissions table is round-3 §2.4. *Rejected:* two places deciding who may create a channel.
14. **Agent posts are the key's creator's** (§6 #12, #25): `author_id` the creator, `api_key_id` set, "via <key>"; `workspace_api_keys.include_private_chat boolean not null default false` for "Include private channels I'm in", set in the key editor (KEYS-1's typed scopes map, its normalizer and its CHECK stay untouched); every MCP read runs as the creator through the app's reads; 30 posts a minute per key; posts through a key count as new for the creator and replies to them notify the creator only through the key's switch. *Rejected:* the null author and "App" badge; a new scopes column.
15. **Every op logs an attributed activity row except the quiet ones** (§6 #15): logged are `chat.message_edit`, `message_delete`, `message_restore`, `react`, `pin`, `decision`, `wrap_up`, `reopen`, `move`, `channel_create`, `channel_rename`, `channel_topic`, `channel_archive` / `_unarchive`, `channel_private`, `channel_delete` / `_restore`, `channel_link`, `announcement`, `join`, `leave`, `member_add`, `member_remove`, `manager_add` / `_remove`, `emoji_add` / `_remove`, with ids only; not logged: a plain send (the message is its own record), typing, read marks, Catch up ops, reminders, follows, client heartbeats, acks and preferences. **No history limit** (§6 #13). **Reading is what you saw** (§6 #17): `chat_members.last_read_id` compared as `(created_at, id)`; `chat_op_mark_read` moves it forward only; `chat_op_mark_unread` moves it back. *Rejected:* the 600 ms "mark all read".
16. **The stream is TanStack Virtual** (§6 #18) in a chat-only `MessageStream` with id keys, a per-conversation measurement cache, sticky day separators, windowed deep jumps, the accessible feed pattern; only this file opts out of the React Compiler if spike 1 needs it; runner-up Legend List 3's web build. *Rejected:* FlashList, VirtuosoMessageList, react-virtuoso, inverted lists.
