Moduo, a task and calendar app for people who plan their day in time blocks and watch the plan fall apart by lunch.
Drag tasks onto your calendar. When a block ends, Moduo asks: done, push, shrink or drop. Unfinished work rolls forward. One tap reflows the rest of your day. No wall of red overdue tasks.
It doesn't reshuffle your calendar on its own; you stay in charge.
Notes, contacts and email live in the same app, so the meeting note's checkboxes are tasks you can schedule.
$12 a month. Free plan available. 14-day trial with no card. Desktop and web.
