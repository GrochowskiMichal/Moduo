## What would flip a no

- If it turned the time and tasks I log against a client into an invoice at the end of the month, so I could drop the spreadsheet.
- A clear promise that my email stays on my machine or is encrypted so the company can't read it, and then the client view actually saving me time every morning past the second week.
- Proper Outlook / Microsoft 365 support for email and calendar, plus a few months of independent reviews saying it doesn't drop or mangle email.
- Seeing that the email part is good enough that I could actually quit my current mail client and calendar app, so it replaces subscriptions instead of adding one.
- A clear local-only or end-to-end encrypted option for email, so my client mail never sits on Moduo's servers.
- If it clearly replaced Spark and Todoist so my total bill went down, and I could try it on one real client for the free trial without moving everything over.
- If it handled invoices and time tracking well enough that I could cancel HoneyBook and Toggl, so it saves me money instead of adding a subscription.
- Seeing it work inside my existing Gmail flow, so starring an email or replying automatically lands the follow-up on the right client without me switching apps, and a friend freelancer saying it actually stopped them dropping follow-ups.
- A one-click import from Todoist and my client spreadsheet that has me running in under an hour, plus it replacing Calendly so I'm actually cutting a subscription, not adding one.
- Seeing another freelancer I trust use it for a year without problems, and it reminding me about follow-ups I forgot right inside my email without me changing how I work.
- If it imported my Notion client pages and let me track hours and invoice clients from the client view, so it could replace the spreadsheet too.
- If it pulled in my existing Gmail, calendar and contacts on its own and had my clients set up within an hour, so I wasn't moving anything by hand.
