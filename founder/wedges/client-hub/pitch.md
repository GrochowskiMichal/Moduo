Moduo, a desktop and web app for freelancers and consultants who run their business across email, a calendar, a notes app, a task app and a spreadsheet of clients.
It puts your email, tasks, calendar, notes and contacts in one app. Turn an email into a task in one click and it's linked to that client. Open a client and you see every email, task, note and meeting with them, without logging anything.
Works with Gmail and other IMAP email, and with iCloud, Fastmail or any CalDAV calendar. Outlook email isn't supported yet.
$12 a month for one person. Free plan with one workspace. 14-day trial with no card, 30 days if you add one.
Export all your data any time.
