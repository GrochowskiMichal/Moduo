## What would flip a no

- A flat price for the whole workspace around $50 a month, with freelancers and clients as free guests.
- A solid set of G2 or Capterra reviews from other small agencies, plus free guest access for clients and contractors.
- Estimates vs. actual hours per client project, built in, so I can see a job going over before it does
- A one-click import of our Trello boards plus a Slack bridge, so we could try the client pages without anyone having to move chat on day one.
- Free client and contractor access to a client-facing status view with a 'waiting on client' state, so the weekly status emails actually stop.
- Built-in time tracking per client and per task, with a simple report I can send or bill from.
- Free guest access for clients and contractors, limited to their own client page and tasks.
- Built-in timers that log hours against each client and task, with estimate-versus-actual reporting I can bill from.
- Built-in time tracking per client plus a flat price where occasional freelancers and clients get in free.
- Built-in time tracking per client and per task that rolls up into billable-hours reports I can invoice from.
- If clients could open their page themselves and see status without me writing an update, I'd pay for that, ideally at a flat team price.
- If it imported our Trello boards and Slack history in one go and showed time or budget per client against the estimate, so it actually helps with the overruns.
